# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own design handoff bundles and prototype materials used to guide production UI work.

## Ownership

- Child design bundles own their own source transcripts, prototypes, and implementation guidance.
- Production app code remains owned by [../src/AGENTS.md](../src/AGENTS.md).
- [lab/AGENTS.md](lab/AGENTS.md) owns the design lab: one study folder per design request (a screen, a component or a flow), built locally into live interactive prototypes and shown to the owner as Artifacts. New design studies go there, not into new top-level folders here.

- Mark `o` (2026-11-02) is the logo that ships, a faithful vector of [references/2026-11-02/logo.jpg](references/2026-11-02/logo.jpg) in that image's own pixels: the letters JEW and 365, the bell and the open book with its text lines are filled outlines traced from it (even-odd, holes included), and the stopwatch ring is the smooth curve fitted to its traced centre line, stroked with a gradient. They live in [jew365-concepts/vector/trace/source-paths.json](jew365-concepts/vector/trace/source-paths.json), written by [jew365-concepts/vector/trace/trace.py](jew365-concepts/vector/trace/trace.py) ([jew365-concepts/vector/trace/README.md](jew365-concepts/vector/trace/README.md) has the command). `SOURCE` in `build.mjs` holds what is measured from the image: the seven ticks from 12 to 6 on the ring's left half, the stopwatch button, the degree ring and the white square that frames the app icon (`square`, written to `mark-o-meta.json` as `icon.frame`). There is no check, no tefillin, tallit, Torah scroll or shofar, no pale outline and no mask or clip path. Colours are sampled from the image: the letters `BLUE_ORANGE.ink` `#002767`, the button `crown` `#0060FC`, the `ring` and `ticks` gradients, and the bell, book, text lines and degree ring derived from `sun` by `sunTones()`; the palette keeps its on-dark values under `hero*`. Outputs: `mark-o.svg` and `mark-o-dark.svg` (the full mark: icon, splash and in-app), `mark-o-icon.svg` and `mark-o-icon-mono.svg` (the same full mark), `mark-o-mono.svg` (its single-colour twin for the Android monochrome layer and the notification silhouette), `mark-o-notif.svg` (a closed ring, a button and 365, shown only on the board), `wordmark-o.svg` and `wordmark-o-dark.svg` (JEW over 365°), and `mark-o-meta.json` (each shape's centre, reach and box, and the icon's frame, read by the export). The board shows the mark beside the reference and on night, then the icon and the notification silhouette through the checks, and the wordmarks on cream and on night.
- The preview variant's red mark (2026-10-25) comes from the same `build.mjs`: `RED_ORANGE` is `BLUE_ORANGE` with `ink`, `crown`, every `ring` and `ticks` stop and the hero palette's `heroRing` and `heroTicks` turned by `RED_ROTATION` (140 degrees of hue, in HSL, so each colour keeps its saturation and lightness and the gradient reads the same way; the navy `#002767` becomes `#670005`), while `sun`, `bg` and the hero background and ink stay. It draws the same `markO` into `mark-o-preview.svg` and `mark-o-preview-dark.svg`, written after the board so every other output, the board included, stays byte-identical. - [palettes-2026-10-24/index.html](palettes-2026-10-24/index.html) is the self-contained palette board the product owner approved on 2026-10-25; [../src/theme/colors.ts](../src/theme/colors.ts) carries its proposed tokens. Gold is the reference; pink, purple, blue, dark and plum each show their current tokens against the proposed ones, rendered on a mock home card, a done card, an accent `Banner`, a primary `Button`, the siddur reader's toolbar and the tab bar, with the WCAG contrast of `text`, `textSub` and `goldText` on `surface` and `onGold` on `gold` for both (the theme test's other pairs in a closed `details`), the card-to-page step, and the `ThemeSwatchRow` change (the palette's `surface` as a ring inside its `bg`, around the accent dot). Hebrew, right to left, light page, inline CSS with Heebo and Noto Serif Hebrew from Google Fonts; it opens as a local file.
- [palettes-2026-10-24/build.mjs](palettes-2026-10-24/build.mjs) (`node design/palettes-2026-10-24/build.mjs`, Node built-ins only) writes the board and [palettes-2026-10-24/tokens.json](palettes-2026-10-24/tokens.json), the full proposed token set of all six palettes in the key order of `colors.ts`, ready to paste. It derives the proposal from `CURRENT`, a snapshot of `colors.ts` taken on 2026-10-24, by the rules carried from gold: each light palette takes gold's OKLCH lightness and chroma for `bg`, `surface`, `surface2` and `border` in its own page hue (so the card is a warmed white with gold's card-to-page step, never `#FFFFFF`) and `tabBg` equals `surface`; `goldText` is a tone separate from the accent and kept where it already is one: darker on a light palette (else the accent darkened to 7:1 on `surface`) and lighter on a dark one (else the accent lifted by as much as dark's `goldText` sits above its accent); `onGold` is dark ink where it reaches 4.5:1 on the accent, else white; shadows are gold's at 0.08 / 0.18, tinted between the accent and page hue on a light palette and a deep page hue on a dark one; the dark palettes keep `bg` and `surface` and take gold's `surface2` and `border` steps upward. Hues, accents, `goldLight`, the status colours and `overlay` stay. The script throws when a proposed palette misses 4.5:1 on any pair it shows or leaves a light `surface` pure white. Edit `build.mjs` and rerun it, never the generated files. Source material: the app never imports it.

## Local Contracts

- Treat design files as source material, not production code.
- Do not modify production UI from a design bundle without also reading the applicable source-code DOX chain under [../src/AGENTS.md](../src/AGENTS.md).

## Work Guidance

- Keep design-specific rules in the closest bundle-level AGENTS.md.

## Verification

- Run `node design/palettes-2026-10-24/build.mjs` after editing the palette board; it fails when a proposed contrast pair misses 4.5:1.
