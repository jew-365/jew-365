# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own design handoff bundles and prototype materials used to guide production UI work.

## Ownership

- Child design bundles own their own source transcripts, prototypes, and implementation guidance.
- Production app code remains owned by [../src/AGENTS.md](../src/AGENTS.md).

- Mark `o` (2026-10-21) is the logo the product owner chose, drawn after [logo.jpg](references/2026-10-21/logo.jpg) and the one that ships. It draws seven ticks from 12 to 6, 365° centered under JEW (`link: false`) and four mitzvah symbols in the ring's opening in a styled ring (`styled: true`): a gradient ring (`BLUE_ORANGE.ring`, stops sampled from the reference along a 150° axis), a stopwatch crown at 12, ticks on the closed side whose ends keep 1.5 ring widths from the nearest letter outline, a check at the ring's top end and a bell at its bottom end (the ring stops at their pale outline through a `mask`), and a pale outline around every letter, the check and the bell (`HALO_GAP`, `HALO_LINE`, a transparent gap, so it works on any background). The tallit is `tallitShawl` (a folded scarf with stripes and fringe), the four symbols are outline-only, and `scaledMark()` sizes them and the check and bell from the ring radius to the reference's proportions. The palette `BLUE_ORANGE` keeps its on-dark values under `hero*`. Outputs: `mark-o.svg` and `mark-o-dark.svg` (the full mark, splash and in-app), `mark-o-icon.svg` (the launcher icon: open ring, crown, ticks, JEW 365° and the check and bell, no line symbols, no outline) with `mark-o-icon-mono.svg` (its single-colour twin for the Android monochrome layer), `mark-o-notif.svg` (the notification silhouette: a closed ring, a crown and 365), `wordmark-o.svg` and `wordmark-o-dark.svg` (JEW over 365°), `mark-o-mono.svg`, and `mark-o-meta.json` (each shape's centre, reach and box, read by the export). The board shows the mark beside the reference and on night, then the icon and the notification silhouette through the checks, and the wordmarks on cream and on night.
- The preview variant's red mark (2026-10-25) comes from the same `build.mjs`: `RED_ORANGE` is `BLUE_ORANGE` with `ink`, every `ring` and `ticks` stop, `halo` and the hero palette's `heroRing` and `heroTicks` turned by `RED_ROTATION` (140 degrees of hue, in HSL, so each colour keeps its saturation and lightness and the gradient reads the same way; the blue `#012B97` becomes `#970901`), while `sun`, `bg` and the hero background and ink stay. It draws the same `markO` into `mark-o-preview.svg` and `mark-o-preview-dark.svg`, written after the board so every other output, the board included, stays byte-identical. [../scripts/logo/export.mjs](../scripts/logo/export.mjs) turns the two into [../assets/preview/](../assets/preview). The board does not show them.

- [palettes-2026-10-24/index.html](palettes-2026-10-24/index.html) is the self-contained palette board the product owner approved on 2026-10-25; [../src/theme/colors.ts](../src/theme/colors.ts) carries its proposed tokens. Gold is the reference; pink, purple, blue, dark and plum each show their current tokens against the proposed ones, rendered on a mock home card, a done card, an accent `Banner`, a primary `Button`, the siddur reader's toolbar and the tab bar, with the WCAG contrast of `text`, `textSub` and `goldText` on `surface` and `onGold` on `gold` for both (the theme test's other pairs in a closed `details`), the card-to-page step, and the `ThemeSwatchRow` change (the palette's `surface` as a ring inside its `bg`, around the accent dot). Hebrew, right to left, light page, inline CSS with Heebo and Noto Serif Hebrew from Google Fonts; it opens as a local file.
- [palettes-2026-10-24/build.mjs](palettes-2026-10-24/build.mjs) (`node design/palettes-2026-10-24/build.mjs`, Node built-ins only) writes the board and [palettes-2026-10-24/tokens.json](palettes-2026-10-24/tokens.json), the full proposed token set of all six palettes in the key order of `colors.ts`, ready to paste. It derives the proposal from `CURRENT`, a snapshot of `colors.ts` taken on 2026-10-24, by the rules carried from gold: each light palette takes gold's OKLCH lightness and chroma for `bg`, `surface`, `surface2` and `border` in its own page hue (so the card is a warmed white with gold's card-to-page step, never `#FFFFFF`) and `tabBg` equals `surface`; `goldText` is a tone separate from the accent and kept where it already is one: darker on a light palette (else the accent darkened to 7:1 on `surface`) and lighter on a dark one (else the accent lifted by as much as dark's `goldText` sits above its accent); `onGold` is dark ink where it reaches 4.5:1 on the accent, else white; shadows are gold's at 0.08 / 0.18, tinted between the accent and page hue on a light palette and a deep page hue on a dark one; the dark palettes keep `bg` and `surface` and take gold's `surface2` and `border` steps upward. Hues, accents, `goldLight`, the status colours and `overlay` stay. The script throws when a proposed palette misses 4.5:1 on any pair it shows or leaves a light `surface` pure white. Edit `build.mjs` and rerun it, never the generated files. Source material: the app never imports it.
