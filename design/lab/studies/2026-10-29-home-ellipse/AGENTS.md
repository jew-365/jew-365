# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- The home screen study of 2026-10-29, built for one question from the owner: "take today's home screen and show it exactly as it is, except that the half circle of the day's arc and the half of the night are connected into one horizontal ellipse. I want to see how it will look."
- Three boards on the same screen, shown side by side in one gallery page (a next round of the same study, 2026-10-29: the owner asked for a regular circle as a third example):
  - א `HomeNow` ([boards/HomeNow.html](boards/HomeNow.html)): the real app's home as it is today, rebuilt from the app code and measured against the running web app.
  - ב `HomeEllipse` ([boards/HomeEllipse.html](boards/HomeEllipse.html)): the same board with only the arc scene changed, one closed ellipse instead of the day arc and the small night arc.
  - ג `HomeCircle` ([boards/HomeCircle.html](boards/HomeCircle.html)): the same again with a regular circle (rx = ry) instead of the ellipse.

- [core.js](core.js), [core.css](core.css) and [data.json](data.json) are copies of the [2026-10-28-home-mix](../2026-10-28-home-mix/AGENTS.md) core with these changes: [gen-data.mjs](gen-data.mjs) now floors every time to the minute, as the app's `clockOf()` does, so zmanim, candle lighting and exit read the app's own minutes, and adds the Hebrew date with nikud (`hebFull`, `hebNightFull`) and without the year (`hebLbl`, `hebNightLbl`) that the app's date line and hilula rows print.

- [assemble.mjs](assemble.mjs) writes only the gallery, [site-template.html](site-template.html) is its page and [dirs.json](dirs.json) its three entries; the phones sit in an auto-fit grid (three columns from about 1100 points, two below, one under 860). There is no canvas (the owner's word, 2026-10-29) and no ranking.

- `HomeNow` follows the app, not the older [Final.html](../2026-10-28-home-mix/boards/Final.html). The values it pins, each read from the app code and checked on the running app at 390 x 844 (hero 358.2 points, top chip 350 x 58, logo 52, block gap 17, scene 192, chips 55 high, NowCard 152):
  - the top row is one glass chip across the hero (the bare icon with its white halo at the physical left, the date line and greeting at the right); there is no next-zman pill;
  - the back control is centred over the block, 44 points high, glass at 0.6 with a gold border, and appears only while a time is previewed; it never moves the layout;
  - the chips and the two edge pills are the surface colour at 0.2 with the ink chosen per chip for contrast; the pressed chip is solid `text`, the marked chip (pressed, else the next one) has the gold border, and the strip scrolls to centre it;
  - My day opens on the rail; its order is open, then upcoming, then done, then missed; texts use a plain hyphen.
- The board scales its scene by `K = 192 / 216`, the app's `sceneScale()` on an 844 point screen.
- `HomeEllipse` and `HomeCircle` change only what `geo()` and a few lines of `renderVals()` read through `SHAPE` (`ELLIPSE` is true for both). The bullets below describe the ellipse; the circle differs in its radius only:
  - one ellipse: `RX = 142` units (126.2 points, the app's day arc width) and `RY = 112` units (99.6 points) for both halves, centred on the horizon at 158 units, so the horizon runs through its centre; wider than tall;
  - the stage is the full 390 point width, so the two edge pills sit outside the ellipse at the horizon ends (8 points from the screen edge, the app's top) and never meet the lower half; the stars, clouds, hills, land gradient, horizon and hint stay where the app has them;
  - the clock plate stays where the app has it, centred 66 units from the scene top and wholly above the horizon, because its dark daytime ink needs the light sky and the lower half is the dark land gradient;
  - the scene is 286 units (254.2 points) high, which makes the hero 420.4 points, under half of 844;
  - the night half is drawn on the land by day and night alike: its dotted track and the moon's progress are the moon colour, because the app's ink-coloured night track vanishes on the dark land in daytime, and the day half is lit in full once night begins;
  - one drag model: the same `timeAtPoint()` rule as the app, so the sun crosses the left end (tzeit) smoothly into the moon on the lower half; at the right end the app day ends, so the drag holds at alot of the next day instead of wrapping.
- `HomeCircle` takes `RX = RY = 112` units (99.6 points, a 199 point wide circle) on the same centre, plate, pills, chips and back control as the ellipse. Why 112: it is the largest radius that keeps the apex 46 units under the scene top, so the back control still clears the sun and the top tick by about 11 points, and the hero at 420.4 points, under half of 844; the circle is narrower than the app's day arc (284 units, 252 points), so the clock plate (205 points) is wider than the circle and covers its upper sides. The other option, `RX = RY = 142` (the day arc's width, 252 points across): with the centre kept at 158 units the scene is 316 units and the hero 447.2 points, over half a screen, and the apex lands 16 points under the scene top, where the back control and the top tick overlap; keeping the app's clearance moves the centre to 188 units, which makes the hero 473.8 points.

## Work Guidance

- Differences from the real app that stay on purpose or could not be closed:
  - the web build of the app puts the alot and tzeit pills on the wrong sides (react-native-web turns `start` into `left`) and does not scroll the chips to the marked chip; the board draws what native RTL does, alot at the right and the strip centred;
  - at night the app lights the day arc by the night's fraction, so a partial day arc stays lit; `HomeNow` repeats it, `HomeEllipse` and `HomeCircle` light the day half in full;
  - the lab's demo marks, streak and Shabbat block replace the user's real ones; banners and the real marks' late-mark question are not drawn, a mark uses the core's toast; the hilula row shows whenever the day has one although the app shows it only with its switch on;
  - the animation is a plain fade and rise, not the in-view Reveal; there is no display size, status-bar strip or long-press sheet.
- DC template rules that bit here: a text hole renders as `span.sc-interp`, so every style on an element that holds a hole is a child selector (`.edge > b`, never `.edge b`); a hidden Browser pane never runs `requestAnimationFrame`, so a drag test replaces it with `setTimeout` first; add a `?v=N` to a page URL to defeat the server's cache after a rebuild.
- Edge started for a screenshot is closed by [../../tools/shot.mjs](../../tools/shot.mjs) itself.
