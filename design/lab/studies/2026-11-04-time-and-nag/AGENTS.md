# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- The design study of 2026-11-04 for two of the owner's requests of that day, before any production code:
  - a mitzvah reminder that says how long is left until its time ends, with a colour that shows how close the end is ("ספרות הזמן שנשאר יצבעו בצבע של ההתראה ירוק אדום וכתום", in a classic, clean direction);
  - a subtle mark on home, on or beside each mitzvah, of its nagging level.
- Two surfaces, each a pick of one direction, on real Jerusalem data of Wednesday 4 November 2026, and the final result of both:
  - round 3 (the gallery's first two sections and the canvas's first two rows), the owner's notes on the final result: an urgency mark for the iPhone, which takes no colour (5 boards, `NotifR3*`: א ⚠️ at red, ב ❗ at orange and ‼️ at red, ג the word "דחוף" at red, ד ⏰ before the title at red, ה Apple's own Time Sensitive level at red, a build), each on the iPhone only while Android keeps ז; and the bell, whose number was off centre, crowded and in another colour (4 boards, `HomeB3*`: א a soft filled bell with outline and number in `goldText`, ב a solid `goldText` bell with the number in the card's colour, ג an outline bell with a round count badge, ד a soft pill of a small bell and the number). Not picked yet;
  - the final result (2 boards, `NotifFinal` and `HomeTotal`, the gallery's first section and the canvas's first row): the notification as the app sends it after the owner's last word (ז exactly as drawn, with only the small icon tinted, as Android 12 and later draw it) and home's bell with the total inside it, the app's [NagBell](../../../../src/components/NagBell.tsx) at its own sizes (11px bold, the app's, under the lab's 12px floor on purpose);
  - the notification (11 boards under [boards/notif/](boards/notif)): the day's reminders as they reach an Android shade or an iPhone lock screen, worded by each direction; ten need no build, and יא (`NotifLive`) is the idea for a later build;
  - round 2 (4 boards, `NotifR2*`, the gallery's first section and the canvas's first row): the owner's question of the same day on what opens the time line once the coloured dot was dropped, each on the app's own line (`notifications.timeLeft.*`, the pie and the soft tones of [../../../../src/utils/timeLeft.ts](../../../../src/utils/timeLeft.ts)) and with only the small icon tinted on Android, as Android 12 and later draw it: א the pie ◔ ◑ ◕ (what the app does now), ב no mark and "הזמן כמעט נגמר" at the end of a red line, ג no mark at all, ד the pie on the iPhone only;
  - home (8 boards under [boards/home/](boards/home)): the app's home with the level beside the mitzvah's name in the open card and in every My day look.
- The notification directions of round 1 (א to יא): א the recommended combination (a coloured dot by the time, the end time that never goes stale, words instead of a number at red, the Android accent colour), ב a coloured dot, ג a coloured meter of five squares, ד a quiet meter (▰▱), ה words instead of colour, ו an hourglass, ז the Android accent colour alone, ח the time in the title, ט the end time only, י the share of the time left, יא live with a build (a running countdown in coloured digits, a progress bar, the iPhone's Live Activity and Dynamic Island).
- The home directions (א to ח): א a bell and a dot per repeat, ב a bell with sound waves, ג signal bars (always shown), ד a bell and ×n, ה a soft label "3 פעמים", ו dots alone, ז a small dial, ח only on a mitzvah whose own level differs from the app's.

## Ownership

- [gen-notif.mjs](gen-notif.mjs) writes [data/notif.json](data/notif.json) with the app's own code through [ts-hooks.mjs](ts-hooks.mjs) (copied from the [2026-11-01-nagging](../2026-11-01-nagging/AGENTS.md) study): the windows of the default daily set from the registry's `computeWindow()` on `ZmanimService` zmanim, each default reminder's trigger (a mirror of the planner's `buildTriggerTime()`), the repeats of levels 2 to 4 from `nagFor()` and `repeatOffsets()` of [../../../../src/utils/nagging.ts](../../../../src/utils/nagging.ts), every main and repeat kept only when the app's `reminderFires()` lets it out; the palettes of [../../../../src/theme/colors.ts](../../../../src/theme/colors.ts), the notification silhouette and the app icon of [../../../jew365-concepts/vector/](../../../jew365-concepts/vector), the notification colour of [../../../../app.json](../../../../app.json) and the copy of [../../../../src/i18n/he.json](../../../../src/i18n/he.json), the app's time-left line included.
- [gen-home.mjs](gen-home.mjs) writes [data/home.json](data/home.json): the [2026-10-29-home-ellipse](../2026-10-29-home-ellipse/AGENTS.md) generator with its zmanim, candle lighting and exit taken from the app's `ZmanimService` (the Ohr HaChaim method of 2026-11-03) instead of its own kosher-zmanim calls, and the demo day 4 November 2026.
- [core/notif.js](core/notif.js) is the notification core, started from the nagging core and rewritten: the moment looked at, the platform and the level, the stage (`stageOf()`: red at 15 minutes or under 20% of the window, orange at an hour or half of it, green before; `before` ahead of the window), the time left as the app's `formatRemaining()` prints it, and every direction's wording in `dirText()` (round 2 in its `r2*` branch and the final board in its `final` branch, both tinting only the small icon and cutting the iPhone's subtitle to one line, as the phones do). [core/notif.css](core/notif.css) is the nagging core's CSS with Noto Sans Symbols 2 behind Rubik, loaded for ◔ ◑ ◕ only, so the pie shows at the size Android draws it. [core/home.js](core/home.js) and [core/home.css](core/home.css) are the home-ellipse core, with `theme=dark` read as its night palette.
- [notif-base.html](notif-base.html) is the one source of the ten generated notification boards and [home-base.html](home-base.html) (the home-ellipse `HomeNow` board) of the eight home boards; [gen-notif-boards.mjs](gen-notif-boards.mjs) and [gen-home-boards.mjs](gen-home-boards.mjs) write them (the home generator adds the level slot after the name and `nagOf()`, and the final board's totals per mitzvah and level from [data/notif.json](data/notif.json)). [boards/notif/NotifLive.html](boards/notif/NotifLive.html) was started from [notif-base.html](notif-base.html) and is kept by hand. Never edit a generated board.
- [build.mjs](build.mjs) runs both generators and [build-notif.mjs](build-notif.mjs) (the nagging build) and [build-home.mjs](build-home.mjs) (the home-ellipse build) into the build output's `project` folder. [assemble.mjs](assemble.mjs) writes the gallery through [site-template.html](site-template.html) and the canvas from [dirs.json](dirs.json). [check.mjs](check.mjs) is the study's check. [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) is the contract for agents who change a board.

- The day: Wednesday 4 November 2026 (כ״ד חשון תשפ״ז) in Jerusalem, nusach Sefard, the Shema by the GRA. Shacharit 05:58 to 09:32, the morning Shema 05:58 to 08:37, Mincha 11:53 to 16:52, Maariv and the bedtime Shema 17:05 to 04:47.
- The demo's own values, not the app's: the moments looked at (06:00 to 23:02), the home demo's own levels (the morning Shema 4, Shacharit 3, the bedtime Shema 2) and the stage thresholds, which are this study's proposal.
- Findings the boards show: a notification is worded when it arrives and never changes, so a "נשארו" number goes stale while it sits in the tray (at 08:50 the Shema reminder of 08:07 still reads "נשארו 30 דק'" after its time ended at 08:37); the end time ("עד 08:37") stays true, which is why most directions carry it. Several registry bodies already state the minutes left ("נותרו 30 דק' לק"ש", "נותרה שעה למנחה"); a direction that adds a time line makes those numbers redundant.
- What needs a build: colouring digits inside the text, a running countdown, a progress bar, removing a notification when its time ends, and the iPhone's Live Activity (יא). Everything else uses what the installed expo-notifications already sends (`subtitle`, Android's `subText` and the iPhone's subtitle, and Android's `color`), so it ships as an update.
