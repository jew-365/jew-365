# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- The home screen study of 2026-11-08, for the owner's request "לוח זמנים נוכחי מצומצם רלוונטי חכם פר תקופה למסך הבית": a current schedule on home that is condensed, relevant and smart about the period, read both as the part of the day and as the time of year. [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) holds the request and the shared rules.
- Round 2 (2026-11-08), after the owner said round 1's four schedule cards went too far: one small board in three layers, the owner's words "חלק של זמנים קבועים ... ועוד מרכיב של זמנים משתנים חכמים בהתאם לתקופה או לשעה ביום, ועוד מרכיב חכם שהוא מזהה מה רלוונטי עבור אותו למשתמש ספציפי שהוא יכול לבחור להוסיף ללוח":
  - fixed: four times always shown (הנץ, סוף ק״ש, שקיעה, צאה״כ), the next one marked;
  - now: up to two times the hour and the period add (from `schedPlan()`), or one period badge when no time does;
  - mine: the times the user pinned, from a sheet of suggestions read off the user's enabled mitzvot and marks ("מומלץ לך", each with its reason) and the other zmanim ("עוד זמנים").
- The same board in three placements, and today's home for comparison:
  - א `BoardCard`: a card under the hero, above open now.
  - ב `BoardStrip`: the hero's zmanim strip itself, fixed, smart and mine chips and a + chip.
  - ג `BoardTop`: a slim band pinned to the top of the screen.
  - ד `HomeNow`: the home screen as it is.
- Round 3 (2026-11-08), the owner: "תן לי דוגמאות נוספות יותר יצירתיות עם ניצול שטח מירבי תוך שמירה על חלוקה הקיימת ... מתרחב לבחירה או נגלל או יורד כוילון ... לפחות 10": twelve placements of the same three layers, each layer one colour everywhere (fixed the text colour, now the accent, mine the info blue), first in the gallery: 1 `R3Curtain` (a sticky line that drops a curtain), 2 `R3Island` (a floating pill that stretches), 3 `R3Stack` (one card, three tabs), 4 `R3Bento` (tiles, the next time large with a ring), 5 `R3Shul` (a synagogue board, two columns, all the day's times on a press), 6 `R3Collapse` (the full board that shrinks to one sticky line on scroll), 7 `R3Ticker` (one rotating line, a sheet on a press), 8 `R3Wallet` (stacked cards, one opens), 9 `R3Drawer` (a bottom drawer: peek, half, full), 10 `R3Snap` (a card per layer, swiped sideways), 11 `R3Ribbon` (the whole day on a horizontal axis), 12 `R3Flip` (a card whose back holds mine).
- Round 4 (2026-11-09): the owner picked the bento of round 3 ("אהבתי את הבנדטו") and asked to remove the hero's zmanim strip, keep only the relevant times in view with the rest a sideways scroll away, carry the hero's background down under the bento so it ends before the mitzvah cards, and make the bento and the arc scene smaller. `R4Bento` (`SCHED` `p`, first in the gallery) is round 3's bento as it is (the big next tile with its ring, the four fixed tiles in two by two, the wide "עכשיו" row and the wide "שלי" row with +), the owner's word after a first try that changed its shape: "תעתיק את הבנטו כמו שצריך". It sits inside the hero's `.block` on the land in place of the zmanim strip (`heroCls` `hb` hides it), its tiles frosted; the "עכשיו" row holds the times of the moment (without the next time, which the big tile holds) and, past a divider, the rest of the day's zmanim, a sideways scroll away; a period badge sits above it when one applies; the scene is drawn at `K = 156 / 216` instead of 192 / 216.
- Round 5 (2026-11-09): the owner asked to merge the "עכשיו" and "שלי" rows and to add an edit mode to the whole bento (add a time, place it, size it, change it). `R4Bento` now draws a layout of widgets (`next`, `zman`, `row`; sizes s, w, b, and the full-width row) on a four-column dense grid; "עריכה" selects a tile and one toolbar under the bento replaces its time, cycles its size, moves it or removes it, "+ אריח" adds a zman or a removed widget from a sheet, "איפוס" restores the default, and HTML drag and drop reorders tiles too. The default of each zone is the spec [../../../../.claude/specs/home-bento.md](../../../../.claude/specs/home-bento.md).
- Round 6 (2026-11-09), after the round-5 bento reached the preview app and the owner wrote "התוצאה לא יפה ... להציג בצורה חכמה שמשתנה בהתאם לתקופה, יום או שבוע ... מה שבוחר המשתמש ... קרוסלת הזמנים שכבר קיימת ... ללא כפילויות וחסכון במקום ... תחושת פרמיום ... תעשה מחקר": one rule for six boards, each time printed once. The hero's existing carousel holds every time, upcoming first, then the period's times in gold (candle lighting, the Chanukah candles, the fast's end, Shabbat's exit, Kiddush Levana), after tzeit tomorrow's morning, and the past last and dimmed; one smart line tells only the day and the week (Rosh Chodesh, erev Shabbat and its exit, Chanukah, a fast, Kiddush Levana, the coming Shabbat from Thursday night only, as the owner asked the same day, the season's words in the Amidah, the parasha), dropping any segment whose time the carousel already prints; the user's picks are a star on their chip, and a pick the carousel lacks (סוף ק״ש מג״א, the week's candle lighting) joins it. The boards, first in the gallery: 1 `R6Glance` (a frameless serif line above the carousel), 2 `R6Weather` (line and carousel one glass card, Apple Weather), 3 `R6Stack` (one card with היום, השבוע and שלי, Smart Stack), 4 `R6Live` (no added row: the carousel's first chip is the moment's time with a countdown and a filling bar, Live Activity, and the line moves under the arc's big time), 5 `R6Week` (the week's seven days with a mark on each day that carries something; a day shows its times), 6 `R6Arc` (the line inside the arc's plate). The gallery adds the principle, the research with its sources and eight ideas not built; `R4Bento` (what the preview app runs) and `HomeNow` followed for comparison; round 7 dropped every round-6 board but `R6Stack`, and `R4Bento`, from both Artifacts.
- Round 7 (2026-11-10): the owner picked round 6's smart stack ("אהבתי מאוד את ערימה חכמה") and asked for more in the same card (the parasha with Shabbat's entry and exit in the week, real added value in the day), the live chip that fills inside it, a smart mode that is optional, features that need a build, and play with tones. One engine, [r7.js](r7.js) (`r7Vals()`, over `r6Vals()`), gives four pages in one card: עכשיו (the moment's most relevant time from `schedPlan()`, a countdown only, a bar that turns from gold to amber to red, and what follows), היום (what the day is, the prayer's additions, the weekly division of Tehillim, Kiddush Levana when open), השבוע (the parasha, Shabbat's entry and exit, Shabbat Mevarchim with the molad, the week's Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah or fast) and שלי (up to three facts the user picks: the time to Shabbat, Kiddush Levana, the next hilula, Tehillim, the starred times). Shabbat's times live in השבוע only, so the carousel drops its candle and exit chips, and the live page never prints the clock time the carousel marks. A smart mode (on by default) opens the page the moment needs (עכשיו within the hour of a deadline, היום on a special day, השבוע from Thursday night), an automatic turn moves on every six seconds until the user picks a page, and the ⋯ sheet switches both, hides pages and picks שלי. Boards, first in the gallery: 1 `R7Glass` (frosted), 2 `R7Split` (a ring always beside a paper card), 3 `R7Tone` (the card takes the period's colour and fills on the live page), 4 `R7Dark` (dark with gold and two peeking cards), 5 `R7Lanes` (three rows, no paging), 6 `R7Native` (outside the app, each needing a build: iPhone home-screen widgets, the lock screen's Live Activity and Dynamic Island, Android's At a Glance line, widget and ongoing notification); then `R6Stack` and `HomeNow`. [gen-data.mjs](gen-data.mjs) adds each month's molad from @hebcal/core; the Daf Yomi is not offered, because the app's `getDafYomi()` needs `@hebcal/learning`, which is not installed.
- Rounds 1 to 3 were removed from both Artifacts (round 1's `SchedNext`, `SchedRail`, `SchedDay` and `SchedChips`; in round 6 round 2's three boards and round 3's twelve); their boards still build from [gen-boards.mjs](gen-boards.mjs), and git history keeps the published versions.

## Ownership

- [boards/HomeNow.html](boards/HomeNow.html) is a copy of the [2026-10-29-home-ellipse](../2026-10-29-home-ellipse/AGENTS.md) `HomeNow` board with `const SCHED` (`none`), the `/*SCHED*/`, `<!--SCHEDTOP-->` and `<!--SCHEDCARD-->` slots, the scroll div's `onScroll` and `uiCollapsed` (for `R3Collapse`), `openFirstCls` on the open-now heading and `schedVals()` spread into its values. [gen-boards.mjs](gen-boards.mjs) writes `BoardCard`, `BoardStrip`, `BoardTop` (`SCHED` `a` to `c`), the twelve `R3*` boards (`d` to `o`), `R4Bento` (`p`, with its own `K`) the six `R6*` boards (`q` to `v`) and the six `R7*` boards (`A` to `F`) from it; never edit them by hand. It also holds the `<!--HEROLINE-->` slot before the carousel and the `<!--PLATE-->` slot after the arc plate's caption.
- [sched.js](sched.js) is the engine every board shares: `schedPlan()` (periods, anchors, scores, badges), `boardCatalog()` (the pinnable times and the reason each is suggested) and `schedVals()` (the three layers, the pin sheet, and `BoardStrip`'s chips). [sched.html](sched.html) is the card of א, [sched-top.html](sched-top.html) the band of ג, [sched-sheet.html](sched-sheet.html) the pin sheet, [sched.css](sched.css) their styles. Round 3's markup is [r3-top.html](r3-top.html) (the curtain bar, the island spacer, the collapsing board, the ticker), [r3-card.html](r3-card.html) (the boards under the hero), [r3-over.html](r3-over.html) (the curtain, the island, the ticker's sheet, the drawer) and [r3-layers.html](r3-layers.html), the three-layer list they share through `<!--LAYERS-->`; [r3.css](r3.css) styles them. Round 4's and 5's bento is [r4-hero.html](r4-hero.html) (with its edit toolbar), its add/replace sheet sits in [sched-sheet.html](sched-sheet.html), `bentoEdit()` and `bentoZmanim()` in [sched.js](sched.js) hold its layout and actions, injected at the board's `<!--HEROBENTO-->` slot after the zmanim strip, and [r4.css](r4.css). `schedVals()` adds the next time, the layers, the day's full list, the ribbon's rows and each board's UI state (`ui`). Round 6 is [r6.js](r6.js) (`r6Vals()`, appended to the engine at build time: the smart line, the carousel's order and chips, the week strip and the stack's pages, swapped into `zchips` so the hero's own carousel draws them), [r6-line.html](r6-line.html) (the line, the stack and the week strip, at `<!--HEROLINE-->`), [r6-plate.html](r6-plate.html) (the line inside the plate, at `<!--PLATE-->`) and [r6.css](r6.css). Round 7 is [r7.js](r7.js), [r7-line.html](r7-line.html) (the card in its four looks, the split card and the lanes, after round 6's markup at `<!--HEROLINE-->`), [r7-over.html](r7-over.html) (the ⋯ sheet and the native mock, laid over the phone after the sheets) and [r7.css](r7.css). [build.mjs](build.mjs) injects them and adds the `icA` and `icB` icon macros and the `date` prop.
- [gen-data.mjs](gen-data.mjs) writes [data.json](data.json) from the app's own code: `ZmanimService.getZmanim()` (the Ohr HaChaim rules), `dayWindow()` over `MITZVOT` for ten mitzvot (`win`), `HebcalService.holyBlockOn()` (the blocks, their lighting and exit) and @hebcal/core's events, for 75 days from 2026-10-25. [ts-loader/hooks.mjs](ts-loader/hooks.mjs) lets Node import the app's TypeScript at build time: `@/` to `src/`, types stripped by `typescript`'s `transpileModule`, and React Native, Expo, zustand and asset imports replaced by inert stubs. The app never imports any of it.
- [core.js](core.js) and [core.css](core.css) are the ellipse study's core, with misheyakir named "זמן תו״ט" as the app has named it since 2026-11-08. [assemble.mjs](assemble.mjs) writes the gallery and the canvas into the build output from [dirs.json](dirs.json); [site-template.html](site-template.html) adds a day picker (today, Rosh Chodesh, erev Shabbat, motzaei Shabbat, Kiddush Levana, Chanukah, erev Shabbat Chanukah, Asara BeTevet) beside the shared clock.
