# Voiceover script (Hebrew)

One narrator, warm and young, unhurried. Each line must fit its slot; the captions in [stage.html](stage.html) carry the same words. The voweled column is for a human narrator; [tts.py](tts.py) speaks the plain column (full spelling, no vowels: Phonikud adds its own). A recorded narration goes to `out/vo.wav`, each line at its start time; `node render.mjs` then ducks the music under it.
