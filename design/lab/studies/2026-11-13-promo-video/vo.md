# Voiceover script (Hebrew)

One narrator, warm and young, unhurried. Each line must fit its slot; the captions in [stage.html](stage.html) carry the same words. Record one WAV of 42 seconds, each line at its start time, save it as `out/vo.wav`, then run `node render.mjs` again: the music ducks under the voice by itself.
