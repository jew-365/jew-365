# Hilulot list: candidate tzaddikim (research of 2026-10-26)

These candidates are in addition to the 57 already in `src/data/hilulot.ts`. Every date was read from a page that was opened during the research. **Grave bar** says how well we could show that people mark the date at the grave:

- **Strong:** a news report or organizer page of a gathering at the grave on that date.
- **Medium:** a visit is attested, but the report is indirect or the main event is held elsewhere.
- **Weak / None:** we found no gathering at the grave.

**Two caveats on sources:**
- hamichlol.org.il is largely a fork of Hebrew Wikipedia. Where it is the only Source 2, the date effectively has one independent source.
- bhol.co.il returned 403 throughout, so any bhol item is cited from its title only.

## Notes

**Bottom line.** Of the 29 rows above, 18 meet the owner's bar (Strong):
- Sephardi: 3. Chassidic: 8. Lithuanian: 1. Recent gedolim: 3. Kaduri is counted under recent gedolim.

Another 6 are Medium: Baba Meir, Amram ben Diwan, Diskin, Edelstein, Wosner and Beis Yisrael (see Dropped). The rest are listed for completeness only.

**The Lithuanian gap is structural, not a research miss.** Litvish practice does not hold hilulot at the grave. Only the Ramchal (Italian, but revered by Litvaks) passes cleanly. To keep the four groups balanced, the owner can:
- accept fewer Lithuanian entries, or
- relax the bar for Litvaks to "yahrzeit widely marked" (azkara in the yeshiva).

Shteinman and Edelstein are Lithuanian and are already counted under recent gedolim.

**Disagreements and traps:**
- Amram ben Diwan: he died on 15 Av, but the pilgrimage is on Lag BaOmer.
- Chida: the reinterment date (20 Iyar 5720) is not the hilula.
- Nissim Karelitz: one source says 22 Tishrei instead of 23.
- Brisker Rav: his date is 9 Tishrei, not 9 Cheshvan as first assumed.
- Baba Meir: buried on Har HaZeitim, not in Ashkelon.
- Ramchal: both the grave and the year are disputed.
- Adar rules for a leap year need the rav (R-item):
  - Sonnenfeld died in Adar II.
  - Chida and Tzvi Yehuda Kook died in plain Adar; Kook's yahrzeit is observed in Adar I.

**Holy-block collisions.** The app folds a notice that would fire inside Shabbat or Yom Tov into the pre-block notice:
- Wosner (15 Nisan) always falls on Pesach.
- The Brisker Rav (9 Tishrei) always falls on erev Yom Kippur, close to the block.
- Baba Meir (17 Nisan) falls on Chol HaMoed.

**Abroad (5):** Kerestir, Sanz, Dinov, Ujhely and Amram ben Diwan. A Waze link only helps a visitor who is there.

**Har HaZeitim, access needs security coordination (5):** Baba Meir, Diskin, Sonnenfeld, Lopian and Tzvi Yehuda Kook.

**Spring cluster.** Sanz (25 Nisan), Vitebsk (1 Iyar), Kerestir (3 Iyar) and Chouri (25 Iyar) fall close together.

## Dropped

- **Rabbi David uMoshe.** No known date of death. Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan is a fixed hilula held at the Safed memorial house, not at the grave in Morocco.
- **Rabbi David Abuchatzeira (Damanhur).** This was a mix-up: Rabbi Yaakov Abuchatzeira, already on the list, is the one buried in Damanhur. Baba Dudu (14 Kislev 5680, Morocco) has a single source only.
- **Chessed LeAvraham (Rabbi Avraham Azulai).** The sources give 24 or 21 Cheshvan. The grave site is disputed, no gathering was found, and Hebron needs coordination to visit.
- **Beis Yisrael of Ger.** The date is solid: 2 Adar 5737, on Har HaZeitim. The only gathering evidence is a bhol headline that could not be opened (https://www.bhol.co.il/news/62930). He could be re-added if that page is checked by hand.
- **Rabbi Avraham Shapira.** He died on 15 Tishrei (Sukkot), and the memorial is moved to late Elul at the yeshiva. No gathering at the grave.
- **Rabbi Eliyahu Dessler.** The sources split between 24 and 25 Tevet, and no gathering at the grave was found.
- **Baba Elazar Abuchatzeira.** The time of death on the night of 27 Tammuz is ambiguous, there is one source only, and no hilula evidence was found.
- **Possible substitute, not vetted:** the Rabbi Ezra Attia hilula at his grave (kikar, not opened).
