# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own the public website served by GitHub Pages from this folder: the support page and the privacy policy that both app stores link to.

## Ownership

- [index.html](index.html) is the support and marketing page. Apple's Support URL and Marketing URL point here. It is one Hebrew page: the tagline, a feature list, the support address, the FAQ, a privacy summary and a one-line English summary that names the app "Jew 365".
- [privacy/index.html](privacy/index.html) is the Hebrew privacy policy, the canonical text.
- [privacy/en/index.html](privacy/en/index.html) is the English privacy policy and must stay a faithful equivalent of the Hebrew one.
- [taharah-review.html](taharah-review.html) is the Hebrew review document for the rav who approves the halachic rules of the family-purity tracker. It is reached by direct URL only (nothing links to it, and it is `noindex`). It must match [../src/data/taharahPresets.ts](../src/data/taharahPresets.ts) exactly: a changed preset value, rule or `TAHARAH_RULES_VERSION` changes the table, the rules version in its meta line and the answer form's items in the same edit. Section 8 is that answer form: each of the 14 items states what the app does today, with a נכון / לא נכון choice and a note (required for לא נכון), the rav's name required and contact optional, a draft kept in the browser's localStorage, and one submit that posts JSON to the Formspree form `xwlvgagp` ("taharah rav review" on 365jew@gmail.com, CAPTCHA off for the page's fetch), with a mailto fallback when the post fails. Its `RULES_VERSION` and item texts follow the presets like the rest of the page. It is a review document, not a user-facing policy, and not a claim about shipped behavior.
- [halacha-review.html](halacha-review.html) is the Hebrew review page for the rav who approves the halachic rules of the 2026-10-24 batch, R1 to R10 under For the rav in [../not-close.md](../not-close.md), each as the code implements it. It is reached by direct URL only (nothing links to it, and it is `noindex`). Section 1 states the zmanim those rules use, as [../src/services/ZmanimService.ts](../src/services/ZmanimService.ts) and [../src/services/HebcalService.ts](../src/services/HebcalService.ts) compute them. Section 2 is the answer form, built like the taharah page's: 17 items (`R1` to `R7`, R8 split into `R8.1` to `R8.8`, `R9` and `R10`), each with its question, what the app does today, its `מקור` line (the halachic text or source siddur the rule rests on, or a plain statement that the app decided and no source was checked) and its `מקום בקוד` line (where the rule lives, in one place or several), a נכון / לא נכון choice and a note (required for לא נכון), the rav's name required and contact optional, a draft kept in the browser's localStorage, and one submit that posts JSON to the same Formspree form `xwlvgagp` under the subject `תשובת הרב להחלטות ההלכתיות`, with a mailto fallback when the post fails. Its form styles and script are copies of the taharah page's (only the draft key and the subject differ), so a fix to one is made in both. A `מקור` line cites only a text that was opened and checked (Shulchan Aruch and Mishnah Berurah, the Talmud, the pinned siddur sources, Peninei Halakhah and the Chabad pages), never one written from memory. A change to any of these rules (its code, its R entry, or the Ushpizin entry once it ships) changes its item, `RULES_VERSION` in its script and the version in its meta line in the same edit, and a rav's answer comes back as a change to that one rule and its item. It is a review document, not a user-facing policy.
- [testers/index.html](testers/index.html) is the Hebrew invitation to test the app, the web version of the WhatsApp invitation in [../release/PLAY_CONSOLE_METADATA.md](../release/PLAY_CONSOLE_METADATA.md): what the app offers, why to join, the Android closed test's three steps with their links (the Google Group, the Play opt-in page, the store listing), the "Item not found" fallback and the 14-day rule, the iPhone's two TestFlight steps (the TestFlight app on the App Store, the external group's public link), and how to send feedback. It is `noindex` and reached by the link sent to testers. Its links and steps change together with that invitation.
- [style.css](style.css) is shared by every page here, [taharah-review.html](taharah-review.html), [halacha-review.html](halacha-review.html) and [testers/index.html](testers/index.html) included, and is the only place a colour lives: the review pages keep no tokens of their own beyond their print blocks. Its tokens mirror the app's default and dark palettes (`T_LIGHT` and `T_DARK` in [../src/theme/colors.ts](../src/theme/colors.ts)): light background `#F6F1E8`, card `#FFFDF9`, text `#1D2240`, accent `#F08A3C`, accent text `#A04A0C`, peach note fill `#FDE9D5`; dark background `#0E1626`, card `#172238`, text `#EEF1F7`, accent `#F59A4A`, accent text `#F7A65E`, note fill `#3A2D26` (`goldLight`). An app palette change changes these tokens in the same edit, and every text on its fill stays at 4.5:1 or better.
- [favicon.svg](favicon.svg) is the tab icon every page links first: the light and dark marks in one file, switched by `prefers-color-scheme`, so the icon stays visible on a dark browser theme. [favicon.png](favicon.png), a copy of [../assets/favicon.png](../assets/favicon.png), is the 48 px fallback linked beside it for a browser without SVG icons. `pnpm logo:build` writes both, so never replace either by hand.
- [logo.svg](logo.svg) and [logo-dark.svg](logo-dark.svg) are the vector mark for light and dark backgrounds, copies of `mark-o.svg` and `mark-o-dark.svg` that `pnpm logo:build` writes. A page that shows the logo uses them through a `<picture>` with a `prefers-color-scheme: dark` source, never the 48 px favicon, which blurs on a high-density screen.
- [fonts/](fonts) holds the Heebo Regular and Bold files [style.css](style.css) serves. Their licence is [fonts/OFL.txt](fonts/OFL.txt), the SIL Open Font License 1.1 with the copyright line the font files carry; it stays beside the fonts, because the licence requires it to travel with every copy.
- [chok/](chok) is the Chok LeYisrael add-on the app downloads, generated by `pnpm chok:build` ([../scripts/chok/AGENTS.md](../scripts/chok/AGENTS.md)): a manifest, per parasha one content-addressed JSON and its content-addressed gzip (`.json.gz`), and [chok/LICENSE.txt](chok/LICENSE.txt); never edit a file there.
- [guide/](guide) is the user guide's web copy, generated by `pnpm guide:build` ([../scripts/AGENTS.md](../scripts/AGENTS.md)) from [../src/data/guide.ts](../src/data/guide.ts): [guide/index.html](guide/index.html) in Hebrew and [guide/en/index.html](guide/en/index.html) in English, on [style.css](style.css); [../src/data/__tests__/guide.test.ts](../src/data/__tests__/guide.test.ts) fails when they differ from what the build writes now, so never edit a file there. The support page links both, and each claim in them must hold for the shipped code.
- [selichot/](selichot) is the Selichot add-on the app downloads per nusach, generated by `pnpm siddur:build` ([../scripts/siddur/AGENTS.md](../scripts/siddur/AGENTS.md)): [selichot/manifest.json](selichot/manifest.json) and one content-addressed gzip per nusach; never edit a file there.

## Local Contracts

- The support page and the privacy policies are public and legally binding. Every claim must be provable from the shipped code, not from intent.
- The policy names every third party the app talks to. Today that is Expo (EAS Update: a technical update check carrying a random installation identifier Expo counts installs by, no personal data), Formspree (Formspree, Inc., formspree.io: only a message the user sends from Settings → About and contact, carrying the name, phone and message typed there plus the app version, the OS and its version and the UI language, exactly the body `sendContactMessage()` in [../src/services/ContactService.ts](../src/services/ContactService.ts) posts), GitHub (GitHub, Inc.): hosts this site, the Chok LeYisrael and Selichot files included; the app contacts it only to download or update an add-on at the user's request, and, while one is installed, for at most one version check a day per add-on when its screen or the More tab opens, with no personal data; GitHub sees the device's IP address like any web request, and Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io: crash reports from production builds only, carrying the stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and update id, with no personal data, no taharah data and no screen content, IP addresses not stored, and no in-app opt-out). Adding any other analytics, crash reporting, ad SDK or backend call, or widening what Sentry receives, breaks that claim and requires editing both language versions in the same change.
- Both policies carry the same thirteen sections, in this order: what the app stores; profile answers (gender, marital status); family purity tracking; biometric lock; where data is stored; what does leave the device; crash reports; what the app does not do; permissions and why they are needed (location, notifications, exact alarms, run at boot, hourly background refresh, biometrics, wake lock); deleting your data; children; changes to this policy; contact. A new data category gets its own section or list item in both.
- The policy states the backup exactly as the code behaves: on Android the Auto Backup whitelist of [../scripts/withAndroidBackupRules.js](../scripts/withAndroidBackupRules.js) (preferences, mitzvot, history, siddur bookmarks and last reading places, profile answers; taharah data out), on iOS the device's iCloud backup of the app's storage (the encrypted taharah file may ride it without its key; the unencrypted fallback file never does, it lives under `Library/Caches`), and the exported file of [../src/services/BackupService.ts](../src/services/BackupService.ts) (unencrypted, never taharah data). Changing either changes both language versions.
- The policy states how the family purity data is protected exactly as [../src/services/TaharahStorage.ts](../src/services/TaharahStorage.ts) behaves, including the unencrypted fallback file when the key cannot be read. Change one, change the other.
- The reset wording is the app's own: Settings → Backup, restore and reset → Reset the app (`settings.backup` and `settings.logout` in [../src/i18n/he.json](../src/i18n/he.json) and [../src/i18n/en.json](../src/i18n/en.json)), in the FAQ and in the deletion section of both policies. The FAQ paths follow the app's labels the same way: hilulot and Rosh Chodesh (Settings → My mitzvot, the library the row opens, where the "הילולות צדיקים" and "ראש חודש" rows carry the switches; `/hilulot` repeats the hilulot switch) family purity (Settings → Profile → Family purity), backup (Settings → Backup, restore and reset, `settings.backup`; on a new phone, `onboarding.restore` on the welcome step), and contact (Settings → About and contact, `settings.contact`; both policies and [testers/index.html](testers/index.html) name it the same way). [settings.backup.render.test.tsx](../src/app/__tests__/settings.backup.render.test.tsx) fails when the FAQ or a policy names the reset or the export by another path, and [settings.contact.render.test.tsx](../src/app/__tests__/settings.contact.render.test.tsx) fails when the FAQ, a policy or the testers page names the contact section by another path.
- The app opens the `#delete` section of each policy (`dataDeletionUrl()` in [../src/services/ContactService.ts](../src/services/ContactService.ts), the "Delete your data" link in Settings → Backup, restore and reset): keep `<h2 id="delete">` on that heading in both language versions.
- Settings → About and contact opens the support page and the privacy policy of the UI language by `PRODUCT_LINKS` in [../src/services/ContactService.ts](../src/services/ContactService.ts); moving or renaming one of them changes that constant in the same change. Its Chok LeYisrael credit row opens the in-app credit sheet (`ChokCreditSheet`), and [chok/LICENSE.txt](chok/LICENSE.txt) ships with the add-on files without an app link. The app never links [testers/index.html](testers/index.html), [taharah-review.html](taharah-review.html) or [halacha-review.html](halacha-review.html).
- Contact details and store URLs are real values. Never write a placeholder here.
- Both language versions change together. A section added to one and missing from the other is a compliance gap.
- Update the effective date at the top of both policy pages whenever their substance changes, and only then.
- Names: the Hebrew pages use the Hebrew display name in [../app.json](../app.json), `יהודי 365`; the English pages use `Jew 365`, the same as `app.name` in [../src/i18n/en.json](../src/i18n/en.json).
- Plain static HTML with no build step, except [chok/](chok), [guide/](guide) and [selichot/](selichot), which the Chok, guide and siddur builds generate, and no external assets: Heebo is served from [fonts/](fonts) through the `@font-face` rules in [style.css](style.css), so a page view contacts no third party. Add no external font, stylesheet, script or tracker.
