- [ScreenHeader.tsx](ScreenHeader.tsx) is the header of every stack screen, a tab uses [NavBar.tsx](NavBar.tsx). It draws no band: the page shows through it. `ScreenHeader` takes `title` (centred, `typography.title` in `text`, `accessibilityRole="header"`), `subtitle` (centred, `caption` in `textSub`), `onBack` (draws the back pill: `common.back` in `captionBold` `text` after the `backIcon(language)` chevron, 14 points in `textSub`, the `chevronRight` icon in Hebrew and `chevronLeft` in English), `actions` (a row at the end of the pill row, which may shrink so a wrapping group folds instead of overflowing; its callers are the report screen's previous / next week `HeaderPill`s and the taharah dashboard's calendar and settings pills), `leading` (centred above the title, such as an icon tile) and `children` (a body row under the title: the reader's two-row toolbar, the schedule's view toggle over its arrows, the taharah calendar's month arrows). `compact` (with `onBack`, `onCompactPress` and `compactLabel`) draws one line instead: the back pill and the title in `heading`, one line, as a button named `compactLabel` (or the title) that calls `onCompactPress`; it ignores `subtitle`, `actions`, `leading` and `children`. Only the reader's compact bar uses it, so every other header is unchanged. The back pill and `HeaderPill` share one look: a `surface` pill with the `cardSoft` shadow, `surface2` while pressed. `HeaderPill` (`label` and / or `icon`, `onPress`, `accessibilityLabel`, `selected`, `disabled`) draws its content in `text`, turns `gold` with `onGold` content while selected, and draws at opacity 0.5 with `accessibilityState.disabled` while disabled; an `icon` is a `MitzvahIcon` of 18 points drawn before the label, and a pill with no `label` must carry an `accessibilityLabel`, which the props type enforces; the previous / next arrows of the schedule and the taharah calendar are icon-only pills, never a text glyph. `CounterPill` (`label`, optional `accessibilityLabel`) is the one header counter, such as home's done count: a non-pressable `goldLight` pill with the label in `bodyBold` `goldText`. Suite: [__tests__/ScreenHeader.render.test.tsx](__tests__/ScreenHeader.render.test.tsx).

- [SegmentedControl.tsx](SegmentedControl.tsx) is the one segmented choice (`options` as `{ value, label, accessibilityLabel? }`, the last naming an option whose visible label is short, `value`, `onChange`, `style`). Its options are separate equal-width pills `spacing.xs` apart, `radius.md`, in `captionBold`: `surface2` with a `text` label, and `gold` with an `onGold` label while chosen; it has one look on the page, on a card and in a sheet. Each option is a `radio` whose chosen state goes out twice: `accessibilityState` (`selected` and `checked`) for native and `aria-checked` for the web, because react-native-web 0.21 maps only the `aria-*` props to the DOM and ignores `accessibilityState`. The generic `T extends string` keeps `onChange` typed to the option values. With `animated` (default false; only the history tab passes it) the options are transparent over an absolutely filled row of `surface2` cells, and one `gold` indicator (`testID` `segmented-indicator`, one segment wide, `radius.md`) slides by `translateX` of `index * (segment + spacing.xs)` in `motion.indicatorMs` with the ease-in-out curve, mirrored in a mirrored row (`useRowMirrored()`); its edge is `left`/`right` on web and `start` on native, and it moves at once under reduced motion or before the first measure. Without it the styles are unchanged. Suite: [__tests__/SegmentedControl.render.test.tsx](__tests__/SegmentedControl.render.test.tsx).
- [ConfirmDialog.tsx](ConfirmDialog.tsx) is the one confirmation modal: `visible`, `title`, `body`, `confirmLabel`, `cancelLabel` (default `common.cancel`), `destructive` (an `urgent` confirm button), `busy` (disables both buttons), `onConfirm`, `onCancel`. A backdrop tap and Android back both cancel. It is a borderless `surface` card, `radius.xxl`, with pill buttons.
- [BottomSheet.tsx](BottomSheet.tsx) is the one action sheet and picker host: `visible`, `title`, `caption`, `onClose`, `children`, and always a Close button of its own (a `surface2` pill with its label in `text`), so a caller adds no Cancel. The sheet is a borderless `surface` card, `radius.xxl`. It lifts above the keyboard on iOS and Android (`KeyboardAvoidingView` with `padding` on every platform), because an edge-to-edge Android `Modal` no longer resizes for the keyboard. A sheet with a text input needs `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"` on the host screen's `ScrollView` (see [../app/AGENTS.md](../app/AGENTS.md)). `SheetAction` is a row inside it (`label`, `caption`, `onPress`, `destructive`); `caption` is an optional second line under the label in `caption` `textSub` (a row without one draws as before), every row is a `Pressable` carrying the `border` hairline under it and `spacing.xs` of vertical padding, around an inner view padded `spacing.md` on every side with `radius.lg` corners; `selected` fills that inner view with `goldLight` and sets the selected state (the fill is never on the bordered `Pressable`, because Android drops a background's corner radius on a view whose border is on one side only), and `onLayout` lets a long list scroll the selected row into view.

- [Button.tsx](Button.tsx) is the one full-width action (`label`, `onPress`, `variant` `primary` | `secondary` | `danger`, `size` `regular` | `compact`, `icon`, `disabled`, `busy`, `accessibilityLabel`, `style`): `primary` is the `gold` fill with an `onGold` label, `secondary` `surface2` with `text`, `danger` `urgentBg` with a 1-point `urgent` border and label; radius `lg`, at least 48 points high (40 compact), the label `bodyBold` (`captionBold` compact) centred on up to two lines after an optional icon of 18 points (16 compact) in the label colour. `disabled` or `busy` draws it at 0.5 opacity, ignores a press and reports `accessibilityState` `disabled` (and `busy`); a press dims it to 0.85. - [ListRow.tsx](ListRow.tsx) is the icon, title and caption row (`icon`, `iconTone` `accent` | `muted`, `title`, `caption`, `trailing`, `chevron` (the one forward chevron, the `chevronLeft` icon in Hebrew and `chevronRight` in English, 16 points in `textMuted`, never a text glyph, which right-to-left text mirrors; drawn when no `trailing` is given; the siddur tab, Settings and the More tab use it), `onPress`, `onLongPress`, `disabled`, `accessibilityLabel`, `style`); without `onPress` it is not a button, and `onLongPress` passes straight through to the `Pressable`. With `trailing` it is a plain `View` (`flexDirection: 'row'`, `alignItems: 'stretch'`; it takes `style` and the disabled opacity, so a disabled row dims its trailing node too) holding two siblings: the `Pressable` with the tile, title and caption (`flex: 1`, padded `spacing.lg` at the start, top and bottom and `spacing.md` at the end), which alone takes `onPress`, `onLongPress`, `disabled`, the role, the label and the ripple, and a plain wrapper `View` around the trailing node (centred, padded `spacing.lg` at the end). A trailing `Switch` is therefore never inside the row's button: VoiceOver and TalkBack reach the row and the switch as two elements, each with its own name, react-native-web renders no form control inside a `<button>`, and a click on the switch never reaches the row's `onPress`. It draws a default `IconTile`, the title in `typography.heading` (`text`, `textMuted` for the muted tone) and the caption in `caption` `textSub`, padded `spacing.lg`, with no fill and no divider of its own: it sits in a [Card](Card.tsx), alone or in a `divided` one. The mitzvot library, the siddur tab, Settings, the More tab, home's next-up row and the hilulot and Rosh Chodesh screens all use it. Suite: [__tests__/ListRow.render.test.tsx](__tests__/ListRow.render.test.tsx).

## Local Contracts

- Use [../theme/AGENTS.md](../theme/AGENTS.md) tokens and `useTheme()` instead of hard-coded colors unless there is a narrow reason.
- Every literal size in a component goes through `scaled()` from [../theme/scale.ts](../theme/scale.ts), or it stays the same size at every display size: a width, height, min or max size, font size, line height, letter spacing, gap, padding, margin, offset or hit slop that no spacing or radius token matches, an icon or tile `size`, and every named size constant (`const CHEVRON_SIZE = scaled(16)` at module scope, never `UI_SCALE` multiplied by hand). The sizes this file states in points are the values at the default display size (scale 1).
  - **Left unscaled on purpose:** borders of 1 or 1.5 and hairlines, `radius.full`, opacities, flex, z-index and elevation, animation durations and distances, shadow presets, fractions of the window or of a size (`BODY_HEIGHT_SHARE`, `RADIUS_SHARE`, `ICON_SHARE`), SVG `viewBox` geometry and the stroke widths drawn in it (they grow with the drawn size), the logo's generated art beyond the size its caller passes, and the one size read against the device: `IOS_HOME_INDICATOR_OVERLAP` in [BottomTabs.tsx](BottomTabs.tsx) is taken off the device's bottom inset, which no display size changes.
  - **Derived sizes:** a size that other geometry is computed from is scaled once where it is declared, so the derived values agree at every step: `WHEEL_ITEM_HEIGHT` ([WheelPicker.tsx](WheelPicker.tsx): the wheel's height, its edge padding, `snapToInterval` and every scroll offset), `TILE_SIZE` and `BUTTON_SIZE` ([MitzvahCard.tsx](MitzvahCard.tsx): the button row's end padding), `PILL_HEIGHT` ([BottomTabs.tsx](BottomTabs.tsx): the pill's radius is half of it), `SELECTED_LEAD` ([ChokWeekControls.tsx](ChokWeekControls.tsx): the lead above the scrolled-to row, which is measured in scaled layout), and the defaults of [IconTile.tsx](IconTile.tsx) (44), [AppLogo.tsx](AppLogo.tsx) (32), [StreakRing.tsx](StreakRing.tsx) (220), [ProgressBar.tsx](ProgressBar.tsx) (6) and [MitzvahIcon.tsx](MitzvahIcon.tsx) (22); a caller that passes its own `size` or `height` scales it itself.
