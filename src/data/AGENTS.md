# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own static app registries and conversion logic for mitzvot, cities, nuschaot, and custom mitzvah adaptation.

## Ownership

- [mitzvot.ts](mitzvot.ts) owns the central static mitzvah registry. `CHOK_LEYISRAEL` (`chok_leyisrael`, the reader's `CHOK_TEXT_ID`) is always listed, last in `MITZVOT` and switched off by default: its window is `null` until the add-on is installed (`hasChokContent()`), which keeps it off home, history, the check-in and the schedule while the library, the More tab and its own screen offer the download. It stays exported for its suite. Every entry carries its library `group`, and `LIBRARY_GROUPS` is the order of the library's sections (prayers, daily, weekly, seasonal, other, mine).
- [cities.ts](cities.ts) owns supported city/location presets. Every `CITIES` entry is a `City` (a `Location` with a required `cityId`): a stable, unique, lowercase ASCII slug written out once from the English name and never derived again, so renaming a city keeps the id a saved location holds; [__tests__/cities.test.ts](__tests__/cities.test.ts) pins every id. `findCityById()` and `findCityByName()` look an entry up. `cityOf(location)` finds the entry a saved or GPS location stands for: by `cityId` when it has one (an id no city holds finds none), else by exact Hebrew `name`, then `nameEn`, for a snapshot saved before ids existed. `refreshSavedLocation(saved, source)` is the one hydration rule, called by [../stores/useUserStore.ts](../stores/useUserStore.ts): a manual pick becomes the current entry, a GPS fix keeps its own `lat` / `lng` and takes every other field (`name`, `nameEn`, `tz`, `inIsrael`, `elevation`, `candleLightingMinutes`, `cityId`) from the entry, and a location that matches no city (a far fix, "current location") comes back unchanged. `resolvedLocation()` in [../services/LocationService.ts](../services/LocationService.ts) stamps the same `cityId` and `candleLightingMinutes` on a GPS fix within 25 km of a city. A corrected value in `CITIES` therefore reaches every install on its next launch.
- [nuschaot.ts](nuschaot.ts) owns nusach metadata.
- [customMitzvotAdapter.ts](customMitzvotAdapter.ts) adapts persisted custom mitzvot into the shared mitzvah shape. Its category is derived from the hours by `categoryForWindow()` in [../utils/customMitzvah.ts](../utils/customMitzvah.ts) (a stale `category` key on a stored mitzvah is ignored), and its `computeWindow` for app day D (`date`, the device-local midnight of a location date) returns `null` when `appliesOn(custom, dateKey(date))` from [../utils/customMitzvah.ts](../utils/customMitzvah.ts) rejects D, so every surface skips that day like any other `null` window; otherwise it puts the hours on civil date D, unless they end at or before D's alot (`HebcalService.dawnAt()`), when they take civil date D + 1 if those end at or before D + 1's alot, so a window wholly between midnight and alot belongs to the evening before and home, the day view, history, the check-in and the notification ids key it under D (the user's rule of 2026-10-12), while hours that reach past alot (an all-day window, a morning one, `05:00`–`07:00`) keep their own date in every season. D has no window when its own hours are in the night and D + 1's are not (the day alot moves earlier past the end minute, or the fall-back night sets it back an hour), and D + 1's night hours are skipped when D's own already reach past alot, so no instant is keyed under two days. Suite: [__tests__/customNightWindow.test.ts](__tests__/customNightWindow.test.ts). A window ends the next calendar day, at the end clock in the location's zone, when the end is at or before the start (`22:00`–`01:00`; equal times are 24 hours), so a DST change inside it moves the real length, not the wall-clock end; the spring-forward collapse guard keeps `windowMinutes()` of the configured hours. It drops any `bodyVariants` a reminder carries: rotating bodies belong to the registry mitzvot only (the user's call, 2026-10-11), so a custom reminder always notifies with its `label`, and a custom mitzvah saved before that date loses the variants it stored. Its `group` is always `mine`.
- [siddur.ts](siddur.ts) owns the siddur text registries: `SIDDUR_TEXTS` (which mitzvot have a text, whether it is an evening text, and on which days it is unavailable) with `mitzvahTextId()` and `hasSiddurText()`, and `STANDALONE_TEXTS` (texts tied to no mitzvah: name in both languages, `SiddurGroup`, `evening` for a text said at night, an optional `available` condition and the `availableLabel` the catalog shows when it fails, and `availableIn`, which replaces both for a nusach whose minhag sets other days; `standaloneAvailability(id, nusach)` is the one reader of the three, used by `hasStandaloneText()` and the catalog) with `standaloneTextId()`, `standaloneTextsIn(group, nusach?)` (given a nusach, only the texts built for it, `siddurTextBuiltFor(nusach, id)`: a bundled asset of that nusach, or Selichot, a download for every nusach; what the siddur tab, the custom mitzvah's text picker and the guide's texts list show), `SIDDUR_GROUPS` (meals, general, occasions: the order of the standalone groups; the siddur tab draws meals right after now, before the prayers and mitzvot groups, and the other two after them; a group lists its texts in the key order of `STANDALONE_TEXTS`, so the general group reads Tikkun HaKlali, Tehillim (right after it, the user's call on 2026-10-12), Nishmat Kol Chai, the bedtime Shema, the traveler's prayer, Asher Yatzar, the food blessings, the sights, the mezuzah and Kiddush Levana, the user's call on 2026-10-12), `hasStandaloneText(id, nusach, features)`, `standaloneHoursAt(id, nusach, instant, location, place)` (the hours of a text with a time of its own, its entry's `hours`, open at that instant on a day that offers it, from the evening before through the next morning, else `null`: what the siddur tab's now card lists; the bedtime Shema and Kiddush Levana from nightfall to the next alot, Selichot from chatzot halayla to the next `sofZmanTfilaGra`, the user's decisions of 2026-10-18; an entry whose `hours` is `'mitzvah'`, the Chanukah candles, takes them from `dayWindow()` of the mitzvah `MITZVAH_STANDALONE_TEXT` links to it, on the evening before and on the app day, read through a lazy `require()` since [mitzvot.ts](mitzvot.ts) imports this module, so the card opens and closes with that mitzvah's window) and `standaloneTextDay(id, instant, location)`, the Hebrew day a text opened with no date resolves for: `HebcalService.hebrewNightAt()` for an evening text, else `hebrewDayAt()`. An entry may carry `periodEnd(night, nusach, location)`, the instant after which that night's text is no longer said even where its hours run on: `standalonePeriodEnded(id, nusach, instant, location)` tells whether the night in effect at an instant is past it (the siddur tab's row greys out on it), and `standaloneHoursAt()` returns `null` from there and ends its hours there at the latest. `SIDDUR_TEXT_IDS` lists both, `isSiddurTextId()` tells whether a stored id still names one (a custom mitzvah's siddur block may hold a retired id such as `tzitzit`), and every id ships for all four nuschaot (a text the same in all of them, such as Tikkun HaKlali and Tehillim, as one shared asset the generated map gives each), except `ushpizin`, which Chabad does not say (R5 in [../../not-close.md](../../not-close.md), For the rav): Chabad has no asset, so `hasStandaloneText()`, the siddur tab, the settings search and the text picker leave it out there; every id but `selichot` is bundled: `siddurTextPresent(nusach, id)` (read by `hasStandaloneText()` and the settings search) holds for Selichot only while the nusach's file is installed, and `selichotSeason(year, nusach, place)` and `selichotOfferOn(today, nusach, place)` with `SELICHOT_OFFER_LEAD_DAYS` (7) give home's offer window, from seven days before the first night of the Hebrew year's season, by the nusach's own flags, through its last night). It also owns `siddurPlace()` (the Israel and Jerusalem facts the day flags need, and the location the Kiddush Levana flags read, from the user's settings and location) and the custom-mitzvah text adapter. Kiddush Levana's availability is per nusach through `availableIn`: each entry matches that nusach's flag from `KIDDUSH_LEVANA_FLAGS` and no Shabbat, Yom Tov or Yom Kippur night, with its own `availableLabel`; its `periodEnd` is `kiddushLevanaPeriod().end`. A text said once a year carries `season(year)`, the first Hebrew day its season can open in a Hebrew year (`chanukah_candles`, `ushpizin` and `selichot` in `STANDALONE_TEXTS`, `sefirat_haomer` in `SIDDUR_TEXTS`); it only shortens a search and never decides whether a day has the text, which `hasStandaloneText()` alone does. `standaloneReadingDay(id, nusach, today, periodEnded, place)` returns the Hebrew day a standalone text opens for, as `{ day, offSeason }`: `today` (from `standaloneTextDay()`, with `periodEnded` from `standalonePeriodEnded()`) when the text is said on it and its period has not ended, else the first later day it is said with `offSeason` set, found by scanning windows of `NEXT_TEXT_SCAN_DAYS` (45) days (the windows a `season` names in the Hebrew years before, of and after today's, else the days right after today), and `null` when the nusach has no such text or the Selichot file is not installed. `nextMitzvahTextWindow(mitzvah, nusach, location, settings, place, now)` is its counterpart for a registry mitzvah with a text: the first `dayWindow()` that has not ended at `now` and passes `hasSiddurText()`, over `NEXT_TEXT_SCAN_DAYS` app days from today (a `multiDay` mitzvah also looks back its `maxDays`, as `currentOrNextWindow()` does, so a Kiddush Levana window still open is returned) and then over the windows a `season` names, the mitzvah's own text's (`sefirat_haomer`) or the linked text's (`chanukah_candles`, `ushpizin`, `selichot`), the linked ones from the civil day before the season's Hebrew day because a linked mitzvah is keyed on the civil day whose evening it opens; `null` for a custom mitzvah and Chok LeYisrael. Both are pure, so the reader resolves the day of a text that is not said today through them and keeps no day search of its own.

## Local Contracts

- Static mitzvah changes must preserve the `Mitzvah` contract from [../types/mitzvah.ts](../types/mitzvah.ts).
- Mitzvah windows must avoid UTC date shortcuts and stay compatible with [../services/ZmanimService.ts](../services/ZmanimService.ts) and [../services/HebcalService.ts](../services/HebcalService.ts).
- In a static `computeWindow`, ask every calendar question (weekday, next day, `isYomTov`, Omer count) about `dayOf(ctx.zmanim, location)`: the location's day those zmanim belong to, at its midday. Never use `ctx.date`. Its device-local getters read the wrong day once the zones differ, and its clock time depends on the caller (midnight, now, or a rebuild's evening). An evening `ctx.date` already sits in the next Hebrew day, which once erased erev-Yom-Tov candle lighting.
- Candle lighting opens only on the erev of a holy block (the day is not holy and the next is), and havdalah only on the block's last day, plus a Sunday on which the Tisha B'Av fast falls (9 Av, or 10 Av deferred from Shabbat), because motzaei Shabbat had only the flame. Havdalah's notification lines never mention Shabbat: it also closes Yom Tov, Yom Kippur and that fast. Both ask `HebcalService.isHolyDay()` about `dayOf()` days. Every window that opens at night (maariv, the bedtime Shema, the Omer, havdalah) starts at `HebcalService.nightfall()`: tzeit, or the block's exit (ten minutes after tzeit) on a block's last day, so no reminder lands strictly inside the quiet block; the Sunday Tisha B'Av havdalah keeps tzeit, since the fast, not Shabbat, ends then.
- A trigger may fall up to `HEADS_UP_MAX_MIN` (180) minutes before its window's start and never after its end, except a `followUp` reminder, which only the custom mitzvah editors offer and which fires only after the end ([../utils/skipRules.ts](../utils/skipRules.ts)); the registry's own defaults stay inside their windows, so the candle-lighting reminder fires at lighting time only and the pre-block notice carries the lead time, except a `clock` default that lands before its window opens (the bedtime Shema's 22:30 where nightfall is later), which fires as a heads-up. A `blockExit` reminder, offered only for a `multiDay` mitzvah, fires after the exit of each holy block that ends inside the window, never before it opens.
- The Chok LeYisrael window is the reader's learning day: from the day's `alotHaShachar` to `nextAlot()` (the next day's alot, shared with the Omer, maariv and the bedtime Shema), or to the start of the holy block that opens that evening (`HebcalService.holyBlockOn()` of the next day, so candle lighting is never re-derived). It is `null` while `!hasChokContent()` and when `chokPortionOn()` of the `dayOf()` day is `null` (Shabbat, Yom Tov, the Friday of a Vezot Haberakhah week). Its one default reminder is a `clock` reminder at 09:00 on the location's clock (the user's call, 2026-10-23); candle lighting is always later, so it fires on every Friday and erev too.
- `birchot_hashachar` runs from `alotHaShachar` to midday `zmanim.chatzot` (R7 in [../../not-close.md](../../not-close.md), For the rav).
- `maariv` runs from `HebcalService.nightfall()` to `nextAlot()`, all night (R6 in [../../not-close.md](../../not-close.md), For the rav), so it stays current on home and open in history until the alot that ends its app day; its `description` is "לכתחילה עד חצות הלילה" / "Ideally before chatzot (halachic midnight)", and its default reminders stay `start/0` and `start/120`.
- `krias_shma_al_hamita`, the bedtime Shema, runs from `HebcalService.nightfall()` to `nextAlot()` (R3 in [../../not-close.md](../../not-close.md), For the rav) with `skipOn: []`, so the night of a Friday or an erev Yom Tov lies inside the block and is asked in its check-in; its `description` is "לכתחילה לפני חצות הלילה" / "Ideally before chatzot (halachic midnight)", its one default reminder is a `clock` reminder at 22:30 (`offsetMin` 1350) on the location's clock, which never fires inside a holy block; where nightfall is after 22:30 (Paris, Antwerp and Moscow in summer) it fires before the window opens, as a heads-up, and on a motzaei Shabbat whose exit is at or after 22:30 the night has no reminder at all. Its text is the standalone `kriat_shema_al_hamita` through `MITZVAH_STANDALONE_TEXT`. It is on by default (`DEFAULT_ON_MITZVOT`) and the library lists it in the daily group after `birchot_hashachar`.
- `selichot` (R2 in [../../not-close.md](../../not-close.md), For the rav) is a `seasonal` mitzvah of every nusach in the library's seasonal group, on by default (`DEFAULT_ON_MITZVOT`), with `skipOn: []`. Its season is the catalog's own: `standaloneAvailability('selichot', nusach)` matched on `dayFeatures(liturgicalDay(day, true))` of the `dayOf()` day, so `selichotFlags()` in [../utils/siddur.ts](../utils/siddur.ts) alone decides the nights (Edot HaMizrach from the night of 2 Elul, the others from Leil Selichot, through the night before Erev Yom Kippur, never into Shabbat, Yom Tov or Yom Kippur). Its hours are `MIDNIGHT_TO_MORNING_PRAYER` from [siddur.ts](siddur.ts) over the day's zmanim and `nextDayZmanim()`, chatzot halayla to the next morning's `sofZmanTfilaGra`. The window is keyed on the civil day whose night it opens, never overlaps a holy block (so it never waits in a check-in), and stays on home and open in history past the next alot until its end. Its one default reminder is `start/0` at chatzot with three `bodyVariants`; its text is the standalone `selichot` through `MITZVAH_STANDALONE_TEXT`. Its window is `null` while `!hasSelichotContent(ctx.settings.nusach)`, so nothing schedules, shows or judges it until the nusach's file is installed. Suite: [__tests__/selichotMitzvah.test.ts](__tests__/selichotMitzvah.test.ts).
- `chanukah_candles` (R4 in [../../not-close.md](../../not-close.md), For the rav) is a `seasonal` mitzvah of every nusach in the library's seasonal group, between `sefirat_haomer` and `selichot`, on by default for women too (not in `MENS_MITZVOT`), with `skipOn: []`. - `defaultReminders` hold the notification copy and this registry is its only source: `label` is the line the mitzvah screen shows and the fallback body, `bodyVariants` are the three bodies the scheduler rotates by trigger day (`pickBodyForReminder()`); the first variant equals the reminder's `label`, or, for a mitzvah with `bodyParams`, is the label, a colon and a tail holding its `%{key}` placeholders (the label holds none: the mitzvah screen shows it as is), pinned by [__tests__/mitzvot.test.ts](__tests__/mitzvot.test.ts). Copy is Hebrew only; the scheduler builds the English body from the mitzvah's English name (`notifications.timeFor`, or its `bodyKey` with the `bodyParams` values, which a Hebrew reminder reads too when its text holds no placeholder and is one of these labels or variants or the mitzvah's name; any other label is the user's and is sent as typed). - Never build a night-time window end with `Date.setDate(+1)` on `zmanim.chatzot` — that is midday chatzot and yields the next day's noon. Use `zmanim.chatzotLayla` (the solar midnight of the night that follows, before or after 00:00 by season and place) or the next day's `alotHaShachar` (`nextAlot()`).
- `omerDayFor()` returns the count due on the night that OPENS at tzeit of the given day, which belongs to the next Hebrew day: night 1 on the evening of 15 Nisan, night 49 on the evening of 4 Sivan. Do not re-anchor it to the daytime Hebrew date.
