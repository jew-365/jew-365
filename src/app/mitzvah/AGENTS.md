# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own mitzvah detail routes for static and custom mitzvot.

## Ownership

- [[id].tsx](%5Bid%5D.tsx) renders mitzvah details, reminders, content blocks, and detail-level actions, including the "פתח נוסח" button into [../siddur/AGENTS.md](../siddur/AGENTS.md). Its header is [ScreenHeader](../../components/ScreenHeader.tsx) with the mitzvah's [IconTile](../../components/IconTile.tsx) (`iconFor(mitzvah.icon)`) as `leading` and the next-reminder preview as its child, and the delete-reminder confirmation is a [ConfirmDialog](../../components/ConfirmDialog.tsx). Its cards are padded [Card](../../components/Card.tsx)s, "פתח נוסח" and the reset are the shared [Button](../../components/Button.tsx) (`secondary` for the reset), the next-reminder line is `goldText`, back replaces to home when there is nothing to go back to, and an unknown id shows a [ScreenHeader](../../components/ScreenHeader.tsx) titled `detail.notFound` with the back pill instead of a dead end.

## Local Contracts

- Use [../../data/customMitzvotAdapter.ts](../../data/customMitzvotAdapter.ts), `getAllMitzvot()`, or `findAnyMitzvah()` when a detail route must include both static and custom mitzvot.
- For Chok LeYisrael the screen opens with [ChokAddonCard](../../components/ChokAddonCard.tsx). While the add-on is not installed (`useChokStore`), the window and the next-reminder preview are not computed, the active row's switch is disabled with `chok.addon.enableAfterDownload` as its caption, and the reminders card is not drawn.
- For Selichot the screen opens with [SelichotAddonCard](../../components/SelichotAddonCard.tsx); while the user's nusach file is missing neither the window nor the next-reminder preview is computed (the header reads `-`, no text button), and the switch and the reminders stay as they are; both appear the moment the file installs.
- Reminder edits must stay compatible with [../../services/AGENTS.md](../../services/AGENTS.md) scheduler contracts.
- Reminders are added and edited in [ReminderEditor](../../components/ReminderEditor.tsx), a [BottomSheet](../../components/BottomSheet.tsx) (the screen passes `mitzvah` and the user's `location` and `settings`) that holds an inline [ReminderTimingFields](../../components/ReminderTimingFields.tsx): the when-to-send radios with their captions, from `timingKinds({ custom, multiDay })` (`followUp` only for a custom mitzvah, `blockExit` only for a `multiDay` one), then the minutes field labelled by what it counts with its -5 / +5 steps, or an inline [TimeField](../../components/TimeField.tsx) for a set time. Below it come the send-time line (`detail.previewNext` with `fireTimeText()`, or the `urgent` copy Save would give), the label, the skip-if-done switch (not for a follow-up), the error and Save; [../../components/AGENTS.md](../../components/AGENTS.md) holds the details. The sheet's own close button is the only way out besides Save. Save accepts a reminder that `nextReminderAt()` would fire at least once in the scheduler's lookahead, whatever the window shown today (a Friday's ends at candle lighting), and refuses one that never fires with `reminder.errors.outsideWindowToday`, or `reminder.errors.outsideWindowNext` when the window it names is not today's. Rows are listed in trigger order (`buildTriggerTime()` against the window shown; ties, and a screen with no window ahead, keep the stored order). Edit and delete use the stored index, so notification ids never change. Each row adds `detail.reminderNextAt` with `nextReminderAt(mitzvah, [reminder], scope)`, and nothing when it is null. A reminder row's title is `reminderLabel(reminder, name)` (the delete confirmation names it the same way), and its `key` is the index with the anchor and offset, never the label, which may be empty; the row's caption under it is `timingSummary()` of [reminderTiming.ts](../../utils/reminderTiming.ts), the words the reminder editor uses ("10 דק׳ לפני תחילת הזמן", "בשעה 22:30", "30 דק׳ אחרי סוף הזמן, אם לא סימנת"), so the screen keeps no caption helper and no anchor label of its own. The screen renders no `Modal` and no `useQuietBlock()` of its own: the sheet and the dialog gate themselves on the quiet block. A reminder row's delete button draws the `close` icon, the add row the `plus` icon, and the legend under the time window a filled circle `View` in the ribbon's safe, warning or urgent colour, never a text glyph.
- The reminder list is `remindersOf(active, mitzvah)` from [useMitzvotStore.ts](../../stores/useMitzvotStore.ts). The first card of the screen is the active row (the user's call, 2026-10-26): a [ListRow](../../components/ListRow.tsx) with the mitzvah's icon (accent while on, muted while off), `detail.activeTitle`, the caption `detail.activeOn` or `detail.activeOff`, and a trailing `Switch` named `detail.activeTitle`; the settings card under it keeps only the nusach and cycle rows. Under the active row, while the mitzvah is on and its add-on (if any) is installed, comes today's mark (`mitzvah-today-mark`, the owner's call of 2026-10-29 when asked: no preference, so the recommendation): a padded [Card](../../components/Card.tsx) titled `detail.today.title` for the window home shows, `appDayWindow()`, kept by `trackedOn()` as home keeps it and drawn once that window has opened or is marked. Its caption is `detail.today.done` or `detail.today.late` with the mark's time, `state.skipped`, `detail.today.open` with the window's end, or `detail.today.closed` with it; its secondary [Button](../../components/Button.tsx) is `home.undo` (`unmark()` of that window's day) for a mark or a skip, else `notifications.markDone` through [useLateMarkDialog()](../../hooks/useLateMarkDialog.tsx), which marks an open window at once and asks about a closed one. The memo re-reads the clock on the `useNow` tick. Suite: [../__tests__/lateMark.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/lateMark.render.test.tsx). That switch goes through `setMitzvahEnabled()` of [useNeedsReminderDialog()](../../hooks/useNeedsReminderDialog.tsx): switching on with no reminder leaves the mitzvah off and opens the dialog, whose Settings scrolls the screen to its end, the reminders card, rather than opening the editor sheet while the dialog closes. A row's delete on an enabled mitzvah with one reminder opens no confirmation and shows `reminder.errors.lastReminder` (`typography.small` in `urgent`) under the rows until a reminder is saved, the reminders are reset or the switch changes.
- Content block rendering must support text, blessing, link and siddur blocks without breaking Hebrew-first layout. `ContentBlockView` draws a siddur block as a link-style row captioned `detail.openSiddur` that pushes the reader at `/siddur/<textId>` with no date and `from` set to the screen's mitzvah id, so the reader resolves the text for the day in effect. A siddur block whose `textId` is no longer in `SIDDUR_TEXT_IDS` (a retired text) renders nothing.
- The "next reminder" preview is `nextReminderAt()` from the scheduler facade: the first reminder the scheduler would fire, across windows. Once every reminder of the open window has fired, or the window is marked done or skipped, it names the next window's first one, up to ten days ahead and past a Shabbat / Yom Tov block, never inside it. It reads the reminders on screen and the completions and skipped maps, so a mark moves it at once; the screen keeps no filter of its own. The header line and every row's `detail.reminderNextAt` render only while the mitzvah is enabled: a switched-off mitzvah schedules nothing, so it names no next reminder and the switch brings both back. Times print on the location's clock as `dd/MM HH:mm` (`fireTimeText()`). The screen passes `quietHoursOf()` of `useUserStore.quietBefore` and `quietAfter` ([../../utils/quietHours.ts](../../utils/quietHours.ts)) in the scope of both calls, so the header and the rows name the moved time, or nothing when the quiet hours drop the reminder; the reminder editor passes none and describes the reminder's own time. Suite: [../__tests__/mitzvah.quietHours.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/mitzvah.quietHours.render.test.tsx).
- The window shown and the "פתח נוסח" button come from `currentOrNextWindow()`, so a night window still open after midnight wins over tonight's; the button shows when `hasSiddurText()` is true for it (`textNow`) and passes the window's day, `dateKey(window.date)`, as `date` only when the window is due today (`windowDueToday()` in [buildDayTimeline.ts](../../utils/buildDayTimeline.ts): open now, or opening before the current app day ends) or the mitzvah is custom: a location date, which after midnight is yesterday's for a night window, not the device's civil date. A later window (candle lighting on a Wednesday, dated Friday) opens by its text id with no `date`, because a date would offer the reader's "סיימתי" for that later day, which marks it ahead of time and cancels its reminders; the reader then resolves the text for the next day it is said. For Chok LeYisrael it passes no `date`, as home's card does: the reader's own default, the same as the More tab opens, shows the portion of the moment, so on Thursday night it opens ליל שישי, which no `date` can name. Every text opens at any time (the user's call, 2026-10-26): a registry mitzvah that is neither custom nor Chok LeYisrael also shows the button when its text exists for the user's nusach (`textAnytime`: `siddurTextPresent()` of `MITZVAH_STANDALONE_TEXT[id] ?? mitzvahTextId(id)`, and not while its add-on is missing, so Selichot shows it only once the nusach file is installed, and Ushpizin never in Chabad), and then opens `/siddur/[id]` with that text id and no `date`, so the reader finds the next day the text is said. Every push to the reader from this screen, the button's and a content block's, carries `from=<mitzvah id>`; a custom mitzvah and Chok LeYisrael keep their own visibility rule. Suite: [../__tests__/mitzvah.openText.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/mitzvah.openText.render.test.tsx).
- The header subtitle is the cycle and the window as `clockRange()` from [clock.ts](../../utils/clock.ts): an end on a later calendar day than the start or than the window's own day (`window.date`), on the location's clock, reads `detail.timeRangeNextDay` ("05:27 עד 05:28 למחרת" for Chok LeYisrael, the Omer and maariv; "00:42 עד 09:31 למחרת" for Selichot, whose window lies wholly after the day it belongs to). A window whose end is two or more calendar days after its start reads `detail.timeRangeDays` with both dates (`14/10 18:37 עד 26/10 03:44` for Kiddush Levana's period). It says "the next day", not "tomorrow", because after midnight the window shown can be yesterday's. The subtitle and the two clocks beside the time ribbon print on the location's clock (`location.tz`), like every reminder time on the screen. With no window in `currentOrNextWindow()`'s nine-day look-ahead, a custom mitzvah whose current schedule starts after today's app day (`customStartsOn()` in [customMitzvah.ts](../../utils/customMitzvah.ts)) shows `custom.startsOn` with that date (`scheduleDateLabel()` in [customMitzvah.ts](../../utils/customMitzvah.ts)) in place of `-`.
- A registry `description` prints in its own [Card](../../components/Card.tsx) under the time window: `description.en` in English when it exists, else `description.he` (maariv's "לכתחילה עד חצות הלילה").
