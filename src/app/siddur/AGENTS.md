# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own the nusach reader route, opened from the notification "פתח נוסח" action, the mitzvah screen, the siddur tab and the More tab's Chok LeYisrael and Tehillim rows, and the `/siddur` redirect.

## Ownership

- [index.tsx](index.tsx) is a `<Redirect>` to [the siddur tab](<../(tabs)/siddur.tsx>) and nothing else, so a `/siddur` deep link keeps working. The catalog lives in the tab, described in [../(tabs)/AGENTS.md](<../(tabs)/AGENTS.md>).
- [[id].tsx](%5Bid%5D.tsx) renders a text in the user's nusach and language. `id` is a text id: a mitzvah (static or custom, found through `findAnyMitzvah()`), else a standalone text (`standaloneTextId()`). Chok LeYisrael (`CHOK_TEXT_ID`, `chok_leyisrael`) is checked first, so a mitzvah with the same id cannot take its place. An id that names a standalone text, opened without `date` (the siddur tab, the More tab, a custom siddur block), is that text even when a registry mitzvah has the same id; with a `date` it is the mitzvah. A registry mitzvah with no text of its own opens the text `MITZVAH_STANDALONE_TEXT` links it to, resolved for `liturgicalDay(date, mitzvahTextEvening(id))`, with its own "סיימתי". The optional `from` param is the mitzvah id whose screen opened the reader. Suite: [../__tests__/siddurLinkedText.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/siddurLinkedText.render.test.tsx).

## Local Contracts

- For a mitzvah text, `date` is the date its window was computed for, the same `YYYY-MM-DD` the notification carries as `dateKey` (recovered from the notification identifier when the payload lacks it). Without a valid `date` (a bare deep link such as `/siddur/tefillin`) a mitzvah text uses the date of the window open now or next (`currentOrNextWindow()`) only while `hasSiddurText()` accepts that date and `windowDueToday()` ([../../utils/buildDayTimeline.ts](../../utils/buildDayTimeline.ts)) holds for the window (open now, or opening no later than the end of the current app day, `appDayEnd()`); otherwise it uses the date of `nextMitzvahTextWindow()` ([../../data/siddur.ts](../../data/siddur.ts)), the first later window whose text is said, and the reader is off season (the next bullet). A custom mitzvah, and a registry mitzvah with no later window to find, keep the window's date, else the app day (`appDay()`). The choice is fixed when the reader opens and not re-read as the clock moves, for availability and for "סיימתי": candle lighting opened bare on a Wednesday reads Friday's text, off season; maariv at 00:20 is still the evening that is open; mincha at 21:00 reads tomorrow's text, off season; shacharit at 01:00 reads the coming morning's text off season too, because its window opens at sunrise, after the app day ends at alot, and from alot on it is the app day's own, with "סיימתי". The header subtitle always carries the Hebrew date and the occasions of the day the text resolved for, with or without a `date`, all without nikud (the user's call, 2026-10-25): the date through `renderGematriya(true)` and hebcal's labels in `he-x-NoNikud` through `calendarLocale()` of [../../utils/weeklyReport.ts](../../utils/weeklyReport.ts), so they match `siddur.chanukahNight`. The occasions name the Chanukah candle of the civil day whose evening the reading belongs to, `chanukahNightOn()` in [siddur.ts](../../utils/siddur.ts), the count the Chanukah reminder's `bodyParams` names: of the window date, or of `appDay()` at the shared `useNow` instant for a standalone text opened with no `date`, as `siddur.chanukahNight` after hebcal's occasions; hebcal's own candle labels, which name the candles lit at the end of the civil day they sit on, are left out (`omitChanukahCandles`), and so is its "8th Day" label (`omitChanukahDay`) whenever a night is named, so the eighth night reads once; by day, with no night named, the eighth day keeps hebcal's label. Suite: [../__tests__/siddurLinkedText.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/siddurLinkedText.render.test.tsx). Evening texts resolve for the Hebrew day the evening opens, always through `liturgicalDay()` of that date; never derive a mitzvah text's Hebrew day from the clock directly.
- A custom mitzvah's text resolves for the Hebrew day in effect at the start of its window on that date: `HebcalService.hebrewDayAt()` of the `start` of `dayWindow()` ([../../utils/buildDayTimeline.ts](../../utils/buildDayTimeline.ts)), so sunset turns the date as in every header. A window set between midnight and alot, keyed on the evening before, names the night's date (10 Sivan at 00:45, not the 9 Sivan of the evening's daytime), and a window that starts after sunset names the next date; a date with no window (a skipped day, a polar day) falls back to `liturgicalDay(date, false)`. The window comes from the date, never from the clock, and "סיימתי" still marks the window's date. Suites: [../__tests__/siddurReader.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/siddurReader.render.test.tsx) and the bare night case of [../__tests__/screens.smoke.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/screens.smoke.render.test.tsx).
- A standalone text needs no `date`: `standaloneTextDay()` gives the Hebrew day in effect now at the location, or for an evening text the night in effect or coming next, re-read on the shared `useNow` tick, and `reading` (`standaloneReadingDay()` of [siddur.ts](../../data/siddur.ts)) is the day the text opens for: that day when the text is said on it and its period (`standalonePeriodEnded()`) has not ended, else the first later day it is said, off season. `reading` is memoized by the day number, the period flag, the text, the nusach, the place and the installed Selichot file, so it re-resolves only when one of them changes, never on the 30-second tick; it is `null` only when no day of the scan carries the text (or the Selichot file is missing). With a `date` it resolves through `liturgicalDay(date, evening)`, as a mitzvah text does.
- Every text opens at any time (the user's call, 2026-10-26). A text that is not said today opens for the next day it is said, off season: `offSeason` holds when a standalone text's `reading` is off season or a mitzvah text's date came from `nextMitzvahTextWindow()`. An off-season reader shows a [Banner](../../components/Banner.tsx) (`info` tone, `siddur.offSeason`) under the header, above the resume banner, while the text is drawn, and offers no "סיימתי" (a mitzvah text's done target is `null`; a standalone text has none). A mitzvah text whose window on the location's calendar date (not the app day, so the night before alot still opens the coming morning) has already ended opens for that date, not off season, under an `info` Banner `siddur.timePassed` in the same place, with its "סיימתי" filed under that date (`windowChoice.passed`; the user's call, 2026-10-26, after Shacharit at 09:55 opened Tuesday's text on a Monday). A `date` param always wins and is never off season: a dated text that is not said that day shows `siddur.unavailable`, as before. The header names the day the text resolved for; the Chanukah night of an off-season standalone text counts from the civil day whose evening opens `reading.day` (`reading.day.greg()` for a text of the day), in season from `appDay()` at the shared `useNow` instant. While the reader is off season, the frame around an insert added for that day is captioned `siddur.addedOnDay` ("נוסף ביום הזה") in place of `siddur.addedToday`, because the day it names is not today.

- Auto-scroll is one persisted setting, `siddurAutoScroll` with `siddurScrollSpeed`, shared with the Settings tab: the reader's switch writes the same field, so pausing here also turns it off there. While it is on, the list scrolls on the UI thread (reanimated `useFrameCallback` on an `Animated.FlatList`), so the pace holds while the JS thread renders more sections. It moves only when the target crosses a physical pixel: through `scrollTo` on Android and the web, and through an animated `contentOffset` prop on iOS, because iOS Fabric reports every non-animated `scrollTo` as a momentum end, and RN's ScrollView then counts itself as animating for 16 ms and captures the next tap, which would make optional passages and "סיימתי" untappable while it runs. The pace is kept in lines: `SIDDUR_SCROLL_SPEEDS` gives lines per minute for each level and `autoScrollPixelsPerSecond()` turns that into pixels at the text size the reader draws (`textSize`, below), so the pace follows the display size too.
- Auto-scroll waits a second after the text opens, the switch turns on or the reader comes back into focus. It stops while another screen is pushed over the reader (`useFocusEffect`), while a screen reader is on (its page scrolls would be cut short), while the section picker is open, while the bookmarks sheet is open, while the resume offer shows and while a picker jump is landing. It pauses during a drag or a fling and for 0.7 s after it, and for 0.7 s after any scroll it did not make itself: an offset more than 24 px away from where it put the list. On the web, which sends no drag or momentum events, a touch or a wheel event pauses it instead. Every resume starts from the list's real offset, so it never snaps back. Coming back to the app (`AppState` `active`) clears every hold (press, drag, fling) and waits a second, then restarts the frame loop: leaving the app mid-gesture delivered a drag start with no end, which held the scroll until the next manual drag. It stops at the end of the text with the setting still on. Frames longer than 100 ms count as 100 ms, so a stall never jumps the text.
- A list that mounts again starts at the top and sends no scroll event, and a change of Chok LeYisrael parasha unmounts it while the new text loads. `useAutoScroll`'s `reset()` therefore sets the shared `offset` to 0, drops the tracking (`position`, `sentPixel` and the iOS `propOffset`, which would otherwise open the new list at the old offset) and holds the scroll for 0.7 s. The reader calls it while the list is not drawn, so it has run before the list mounts, and from the scroll-to-top effect of a parasha, slot or choice change. Without it the new text scrolled on from the old offset, and the star and the last-position save read that offset, placing a bookmark in a text the reader never scrolled. Suite: [../__tests__/chokReader.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/chokReader.render.test.tsx).
- Only a momentum end that follows a momentum begin counts as the end of a fling: iOS sends a bare momentum end after every programmatic scroll, and honoring it re-armed the 0.7 s pause on every step, so the text barely moved.
- The scroll handler (`useAnimatedScrollHandler`) also owns the drag events, because reanimated overrides a plain `onScrollBeginDrag` prop on the same list; the drag reaches `onUserScroll` through `runOnJS` (it cancels a pending picker jump and hides the resume offer), and `onScroll` reports the offset to JS through `runOnJS` at most every 250 ms (`SCROLL_REPORT_MS`), which arms the last-position save.
- Bookmarks and the last reading position exist for every bundled text and for Chok LeYisrael (`bookmarkTextId`: the text id, or `chok_leyisrael`); a custom mitzvah's own text draws no bookmark pills (the star and the bookmarks) and saves nothing; it keeps the settings pill. Both live in `useSiddurBookmarksStore` and are read only through `textBookmarks()` and `textLastPosition()` with the record's `nusach` = `readyText.nusach ?? null` (`null` for Chok LeYisrael and for the texts every nusach shares, Tehillim and Tikkun HaKlali), so a record matches only on an equal text id and nusach. The `star` pill bookmarks the segment at the top of the screen: with no bookmark in this text it adds one at once; with any, it opens a [BottomSheet](../../components/BottomSheet.tsx) (`siddur.bookmarks.saveTitle`, `siddur.bookmarks.saveCaption`) of a `SheetAction` `siddur.bookmarks.new` and one per bookmark of the text, newest first, captioned `siddur.bookmarks.replaceCaption`, where the first adds a bookmark and any other moves that bookmark here under its id (`replaceBookmark()`: its place and label rewritten, newest again; the user's request, 2026-10-27), the place read when the star was pressed and auto-scroll held while the sheet shows. A bookmark already at that segment is replaced and becomes the newest. Each save comes with a haptic and a two-second `Banner` toast (`siddur.bookmarks.added`, or `siddur.bookmarks.replaced` for a move, also announced to a screen reader) and a label that is `titleOf(section)` in the UI language, ` · ` and the first three said words (`segmentLead()`), stored and never recomputed. The `siddur.bookmarks` pill opens a [BottomSheet](../../components/BottomSheet.tsx) with the last place, when the text has one, then this text's bookmarks newest first as [ListRow](../../components/ListRow.tsx)s; a row jumps and closes the sheet, and a place the day's text no longer holds is disabled under `siddur.bookmarks.notToday`. In Chok LeYisrael a row, the last place included, whose key `chokPlaceOf()` reads is captioned with its place (`chok.place`: the parasha and the day) and is disabled only while that parasha's file is not installed; its press is `goToPlace()`, which crosses to another parasha or day. Each bookmark is a row `View` (`siddur-bookmark-<id>`) holding its `ListRow` and, beside it, the `close` button (`siddur.bookmarks.delete`), which deletes it at once and stays enabled on a disabled row; it is a sibling of the `ListRow`, never its `trailing`, because a disabled [ListRow](../../components/ListRow.tsx) dims its trailing node with it.
- The segment at the top of the screen is `placeAt()` in [siddur.ts](../../utils/siddur.ts), computed from geometry the route keeps in refs: each list item's height by `section.title.he` (`sectionHeights`, from the item view's `onLayout`), each keyed segment's offset (`segmentLayouts`, from `SegmentView`'s `onLayout`, removed when the view unmounts), the list's top padding `LIST_PADDING` plus the Chok LeYisrael list header's height, and the auto-scroll's `offset` shared value. A segment's offset is its own `y` plus the `y` of every `MeasuredView` above it (`LayoutNodeContext`), so every view that wraps segments (the section card, a fold, its labeled body, an added-today frame) is a `MeasuredView`; a new wrapper must be one too. A section never measured, such as one above a far jump, gives no place, and the route falls back to the first keyed segment of the section on screen.
- A saved place is reached by `placeTarget()`: the section that holds its segment key, else the section with its `title.he`, which lands at that section's top. `jumpTo(index, deadline, segmentKey)` adds `viewOffset: -segmentOffset(key)` to its `scrollToIndex` when the segment is measured; when it is not yet, the jump lands on the section and `settleOnSegment()` waits up to a second (`SEGMENT_SETTLE_MS`) for the segment to measure and scrolls to it once. A jump never opens a fold: a segment inside a closed fold has no layout, so it lands at its section's top.
- The last position is saved a second (`LAST_POSITION_DELAY_MS`) after the last scroll report, when the reader loses focus or unmounts, and when the app leaves `active`, and only when the list moved during this open. That flag (`moved`) is cleared wherever the list starts again from the top: when a Chok LeYisrael parasha, day or choice changes and when the list leaves the screen while a text loads, next to `useAutoScroll`'s `reset()`, so leaving right after a switch to another parasha keeps the place saved in this week's text instead of clearing it as a position at the text's start. A position at the text's first keyed segment (`isTextStart()`) clears the record instead of saving it. "סיימתי" (the mitzvah's and Chok LeYisrael's) clears the text's record and nothing is saved again during that open. The record's `appDay` is always `dateKey(appDay(new Date(), location))`, never `dateKey(new Date())`.
- Once per open, when the text is drawn, the reader offers to resume with a [Banner](../../components/Banner.tsx) under the header and two compact buttons, `siddur.resume.continue` and `siddur.resume.fromStart`, when the text has a record for its nusach whose segment or section the day's text still holds. A daily mitzvah text (`mitzvahTextId(textId)` is not null) is offered only when the record's `appDay` is today's `appDay()`; every other text and Chok LeYisrael always. A `section` param skips the offer. In Chok LeYisrael the record may also point to another parasha or day whose parasha file is installed: the banner then reads `chok.resume` with the place (`chok.place`), and a place in the text on screen keeps `siddur.resume`. "המשך" jumps to the place (`goToPlace()`, across parashiyot too); "מההתחלה" only hides the banner and the record stays; a drag hides it too.
- Keep-awake activation failures are swallowed: browsers refuse wake locks in background tabs, and Android refuses when the activity is gone.

## Work Guidance

- Hebrew paragraphs are right-aligned RTL in the Noto Serif Hebrew family; English appears only in the English UI, under each paragraph. Native RN swaps a physical `textAlign` of `left`/`right` when `I18nManager.isRTL` is set, and react-native-web does not, so the reader aligns through `RIGHT_EDGE`/`LEFT_EDGE`, derived from `I18nManager.isRTL`. A literal `'right'` renders on the left of an Android phone in the Hebrew UI, even though the web preview looks right.
- A run in the `t` style (a targum line: Onkelos or the Targum of the prophets) draws at 82% of the text size in `textSub`, in the siddur font.
- The text size has two values. The stored one, `siddurFontSize` on one of `SIDDUR_FONT_SIZES`, is a step the reader's A+ and A− and Settings move along, and it never carries the display size. The one the reader draws is `textSize = scaled(siddurFontSize)` ([../../theme/scale.ts](../../theme/scale.ts), the display size read at launch): every Hebrew line takes it as its `fontSize` with `siddurLineHeight(textSize)` as its line height, the English line and the `n` and `t` runs take their shares of it, and `autoScrollPixelsPerSecond()` reads it, so the lines per minute of each speed stay the same at every display size. Every other literal size in the reader (the 34-point controls, the icon sizes, `hitSlop`, the 96-point minimum of the prayer-mode control, the 420-point cap of the sheets and the 96-point lead of a picker scroll) goes through `scaled()` too; the points quoted in this file are the values at the default display size.
- A segment is highlighted as added today when its condition carries a flag (in `all` or `any`) that its section's other segments do not all share, or an omer day. Consecutive highlighted segments within one block share a single frame with a single "נוסף היום" caption (`siddur.addedOnDay`, "נוסף ביום הזה", while the reader is off season).
- A passage only some say starts collapsed behind a tappable label with a down chevron, in the `optional` and `optionalBg` theme colors. An optional section's label replaces its title. An optional block inside a section sits where the passage is said, and the blocks come from `aloneFolds()`. Expanded text is drawn in `optional`. Expansion is screen state keyed as above. It survives `FlatList` virtualization and resets when the reader closes or the prayer mode changes. A press of the commentary control resets only the Rashi and Bartenura blocks' state.
- With a minyan, a passage only a minyan says stays open, drawn in the `minyan` theme color under a small heading that names who says it. Inside an optional block it takes the `minyan` color. Praying alone it folds behind a `minyan`-toned toggle that carries the same label, and opens in the same color.
- Both kinds of block carry a border on the side where the UI language starts. Like the text edges, the side is derived from `I18nManager.isRTL` and the language, because react-native-web does not mirror `borderStartWidth`. In the Hebrew UI, `borderStartWidth` landed on the left on the web.
- The header paints before the text: the load effect starts the read behind `afterPaint()` ([../../utils/afterPaint.ts](../../utils/afterPaint.ts): the next animation frame, then an idle callback or a zero timeout; `InteractionManager` is a stub on RN 0.81), so the parse of a long asset (up to 668 KB) never blocks the first paint or the navigation transition, and it resolves the text for the day, marital status and voice before it hands it to the screen. `resolveForReader()` caches that resolution per loaded text object (a `WeakMap`) and per day flags, marital status and voice, so a reopened text and the render after the load reuse it. The full header block has its final layout from the first frame, so the load moves nothing and `fold.space` does not change when it ends: while a text that is available loads (`placeholder`), the star and bookmarks pills are drawn disabled, the picker row holds a `PickerCard` titled `…` that does nothing, and a mitzvah of the `prayers` group holds its prayer-mode control (other texts learn from the loaded sections whether they have minyan passages or a picker, so a text with a single section and no minyan passage may shrink its header by that row once it loads). The header's occasions line is memoized by the Hebrew day, so `getHolidays()` runs once per day and language, not on every render.
- The body is an `Animated.FlatList` of section cards, so a long text such as Shacharit paints its first sections before the rest. A section's `headings` (Tehillim's books and days) are drawn above its card inside the same list item, centred, in `goldText`, level 1 in `typography.title` and level 2 in `typography.heading`, each with `accessibilityRole="header"`, in the UI language; a picker jump to a day lands on its heading.
- Each section is a padded [Card](../../components/Card.tsx) whose plain title is `typography.title` in `goldText`; "סיימתי", a make-up's done and the retry after a load error are the shared [Button](../../components/Button.tsx); the fold toggles draw the `chevronDown` / `chevronUp` icons. No reader control draws on a translucent white or reads a `header*` colour: the header is the page.
- With more than one picker entry, the toolbar's first row shows one section picker. Its entries come from `pickerEntries()` in [siddur.ts](../../utils/siddur.ts): the text's chapters (Shacharit: the user's eleven stations plus Hallel and Musaf on their days; Tehillim: the prayer before, "ליום ראשון" to "ליום שבת" and the prayer after; both built by [the siddur build](../../../scripts/siddur/AGENTS.md)), or every section of a text without chapters. Tehillim is one continuous text of 178 sections, so a pick of "ליום שבת" lands through the waiting jump below, as a pick of Shacharit's last stations does. It names the entry the section at the top of the screen belongs to (`pickerEntryAt()`, tracked by `onViewableItemsChanged`), and opens the shared [BottomSheet](../../components/BottomSheet.tsx) titled `siddur.pickSection`, listing each entry as a `SheetAction` inside a `ScrollView` capped at 420 points, the current one passed `selected` (a `goldLight` row) and scrolled into view through the action's `onLayout` when the sheet opens. A pick jumps to the section that opens the chapter. The sheet gates itself on the quiet block and closes on a backdrop tap, its close button or Android back; the reader owns no `Modal` of its own.
- A pick jumps instantly with `scrollToIndex`. A section not yet measured is reached without moving the view: the list lifts its render window (`windowSize` Infinity) and renders the sections up to the target and its neighbours in one batch (`maxToRenderPerBatch` the target index plus `JUMP_NEIGHBOURS`, not the default ten per 50 ms, and not the sections after the target, which render as the user scrolls on; which took 3.7 s to reach Tehillim's "ליום שבת" on a desktop browser against 0.85 s in one batch, and would pass the 5 s limit on a phone), retries every 50 ms, and lands once the target is measured. It gives up after 5 s or when the reader drags, and then restores the normal window. Never scroll to an intermediate section while waiting. On a phone one section can be taller than the whole render window (Petach Eliyahu in Edot HaMizrach is about 12 screens), so the section after it never renders. A retry that snapped back to the last measured section therefore looped forever and locked scrolling.
