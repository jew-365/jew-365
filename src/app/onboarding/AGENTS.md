# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own the first-run setup, six screens (spec [../../../.claude/specs/discoverability.md](../../../.claude/specs/discoverability.md), A2 to A5; the owner's approval of 2026-11-15 of the study [../../../design/lab/studies/2026-11-13-setup/](../../../design/lab/studies/2026-11-13-setup/AGENTS.md)): welcome, nusach, location, "what matters to you", notifications and ready, and the two detail screens one tap away from them, the full mitzvot list and the times of home's carousel.

## Ownership

- [index.tsx](index.tsx) owns the welcome step (step 0). At the top of its scroll content, above the logo, sits the language switch (the owner's call, 2026-11-02, after App Review opened an English iPhone on a Hebrew-only step): a [SegmentedControl](../../components/SegmentedControl.tsx) of `language.native.he` and `language.native.en`, `scaled(220)` wide and centred, in a `radiogroup` view named `settings.language` (`testID` `welcome-language`), selected on the stored language, which a fresh install takes from the device (`deviceLanguage()` in [../../i18n/deviceLanguage.ts](../../i18n/deviceLanguage.ts)). Pressing the other language saves the typed name (`setProfileName`, trimmed) and then calls `setLanguage()`, nothing else: the root layout's language effect flips the layout direction and reloads the app on native; pressing the selected one does nothing. Under the [AppLogo](../../components/AppLogo.tsx) mark (112 points), the app name and its tagline, a padded [Card](../../components/Card.tsx) holds the registration heading (`onboarding.registerTitle`, gender-aware), `onboarding.registerBody` ("both are optional"), the name field (`onboarding.nameLabel`) and the gender: a `field` [SectionLabel](../../components/SectionLabel.tsx) `onboarding.genderLabel` over a [ChipRow](../../components/ChipRow.tsx) of `profile.gender.male` and `.female` in a `radiogroup` (`testID` `welcome-gender`), a press calling `chooseGender()`, and `onboarding.genderHint` saying what it sets. Continue pushes `/onboarding/nusach` with both empty. The phone number is not asked here (it is in Settings › Profile), and neither is the marital status. Under Continue, off web, `onboarding.restore` picks a backup file and restores it with no confirmation through `restoreBackup(backup, { reload: false })`, then asks for notification permission and rebuilds once granted, and only then calls `reloadIfScaleChanged()`; the restored profile is onboarded, so the root layout's guard moves to home. A rejected file shows its `backupFailureKey()` message.
- [nusach.tsx](nusach.tsx) owns step 1: the five nuschaot of `NUSACH_IDS` ([../../data/nuschaot.ts](../../data/nuschaot.ts)) as [ChoiceRow](../../components/ChoiceRow.tsx)s through `chooseNusach()`; Continue pushes `/onboarding/location`.
- [location.tsx](location.tsx) owns step 2 (the owner's call, 2026-11-05; reshaped on 2026-11-13 after App Review rejected 1.0.21 (6) under 5.1.1(iv) and 5.1.5). A padded [Card](../../components/Card.tsx) shows the place and a status line (`settings.locationStatus.*` until confirmed, else `settings.locationFollows` for GPS or `settings.locationPicked` for a picked place; the default city reads `settings.locationStatus.missing`, never "ready" for a guess). Under it `onboarding.locationPickLabel` over [PlacePicker](../../components/PlacePicker.tsx), whose pick saves the place as `manual`. The step's one action is the primary Continue: while `locationStatus` is not `ready` it runs `LocationService.getCurrentLocation()` (the system prompt; a throw counts as no fix), saves a fix as `gps` or else the lookup's status, and then pushes `/onboarding/bundles` whatever the answer; with a place already confirmed it pushes at once and asks for nothing. While not ready, `onboarding.locationContinueHint` under it says Continue asks for the location and names the place the times follow without it. A refused or failed lookup never resets a place already picked. Suite: [../__tests__/onboarding.location.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/onboarding.location.render.test.tsx).
- [bundles.tsx](bundles.tsx) owns step 3, "what matters to you" (spec A3 and A4, direction א): `onboarding.bundles.title` and `.body` over one padded [Card](../../components/Card.tsx) per bundle of [../../data/bundles.ts](../../data/bundles.ts) that has a member this user is offered (`bundleMembers()` over `useCapabilityStates()`, so a woman sees no tefillin or Kiddush Levana and Chabad and Teiman no Ushpizin). A card's head is a `checkbox` (`testID` `bundle-<id>`): the bundle's icon tile, `bundle.<id>.title` with `onboarding.bundles.recommended` after a recommended one, its caption (`onboarding.bundles.learningCaption` for the learning bundle) and a tick. Under a ticked card, and always under the learning one, its members are chips (`checkbox`, `testID` `bundle-member-<capability id>`, `capability.<id>.title`). The ticks are the stores themselves, through [../../services/setupBundles.ts](../../services/setupBundles.ts): a member is on when its mitzvot for this gender are all on, its notice topic is on, or, for an add-on, it is in `discovery.wants`; a bundle is ticked while any member is on. On mount, once per gender answer (`applyRecommendedBundlesOnce()`), the recommended bundles are switched on, so a user who only taps Continue gets candle lighting (not a man: the owner's rule of 2026-11-15, said in `onboarding.bundles.candlesMale` under the Shabbat card), havdalah, the Omer and the Rosh Chodesh notices beside the prayers; nothing is ever switched off by it, and `DEFAULT_ON_MITZVOT` is untouched. A member chip toggles its target through `setMitzvahEnabled()` / `setNoticesEnabled()` of [useNeedsReminderDialog()](../../hooks/useNeedsReminderDialog.tsx) (the screen renders its dialog), or `setWants()` for a learning program, which switches nothing on: its download needs a size confirmation outside setup, so home offers it right after (the `wanted` offer). A head tap on a ticked bundle switches every member off, on an unticked one its `bundleDefaultMembers()` on. Two links under the cards keep the detail one tap away: `onboarding.bundles.detail` pushes `/onboarding/mitzvot` and `onboarding.bundles.times` `/onboarding/zmanim`. Continue pushes `/onboarding/permissions`. Suites: [../__tests__/onboarding.bundles.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/onboarding.bundles.render.test.tsx) and [../../services/__tests__/setupBundles.test.ts](../../services/__tests__/setupBundles.test.ts).
- [permissions.tsx](permissions.tsx) owns step 4, notifications: `onboarding.permissionsTitle` and `onboarding.permissionsBody` (it says Continue asks for the notification permission) over a `ScrollView` holding the quiet-hours card and [PermissionsList](../../components/PermissionsList.tsx) (reading its `onStatuses` for the notification status; its battery row is Android's). The card (spec A4, Q33): a switch `onboarding.quiet.title` (`testID` `setup-quiet`) captioned `onboarding.quiet.caption`, which says a late alert moves to the morning or is not sent, and, while on, two [TimeField](../../components/TimeField.tsx)s, `settings.quietAfterTime` and `settings.quietBeforeTime`. On a first visit it shows the recommendation, on at `SETUP_QUIET_AFTER_MIN` (23:00) and `SETUP_QUIET_BEFORE_MIN` (06:30); once Continue has saved a choice (the `SETUP_QUIET_HINT` discovery hint) it shows the store's. A change that would leave less than an hour open (`quietBoundsValid()`) is refused with `settings.quietHoursError`. Nothing is stored until Continue, which writes both bounds through `setQuietBefore()` / `setQuietAfter()`, then calls `askForNotifications()` only while the OS can still be asked (the list reports `blocked` once a refusal has happened), then pushes `/onboarding/ready` whatever the answer: one button before the system prompt, worded Continue (App Review 5.1.1(iv)), and no "not now" link. Suite: [../__tests__/onboarding.notifications.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/onboarding.notifications.render.test.tsx).
- [ready.tsx](ready.tsx) owns step 5 (spec A5). Under the `check` [IconTile](../../components/IconTile.tsx) (size 64, never a text glyph) and its title: `onboarding.ready.dayTitle` over a `divided` Card (`testID` `ready-day`) of `restOfDay()` ([../../utils/setupDay.ts](../../utils/setupDay.ts)): each enabled mitzvah whose window is open or still to come in this app day, soonest first, as a [ListRow](../../components/ListRow.tsx) with its icon, name, `onboarding.ready.openNow` with the time left or `onboarding.ready.from` with its start, then `onboarding.ready.nextReminder` from `nextReminderAt()`, and the window's `clockRange()` trailing; then, while candle lighting is on and not already listed, the coming one by its weekday (`formatDate()`) and time. With no row (a holy block, a polar day, nothing left today) it falls back to `onboarding.readyList` over the enabled names (`testID` `ready-names`). The link `onboarding.readyEdit` pushes `/onboarding/mitzvot` (never `/mitzvot`, which the guard would send back to the welcome step). A `secondary` Button `onboarding.ready.sample` calls `presentSampleReminder()` and prints its result under it (`ready-sample-result`): sent, with where the buttons are on this phone (`.sampleSent.ios` says a long press), no permission, or a holy block; it never blocks the finish. A padded Card keeps the accuracy notice: `accuracy.title`, `accuracy.responsibility` and `onboarding.ready.noticeMore`, which opens a [BottomSheet](../../components/BottomSheet.tsx) with the full [AccuracyNotice](../../components/AccuracyNotice.tsx). The finish Button learns `NEW_SETUP_HINT` (so this install is never offered the catch-up), sets `isOnboarded`, then rebuilds the schedule, then replaces to home. Suite: [../__tests__/onboarding.ready.render.test.tsx](../__tests__/onboarding.ready.render.test.tsx).
- [mitzvot.tsx](mitzvot.tsx) and [zmanim.tsx](zmanim.tsx) are the detail screens, not steps: no dots, and their Continue goes back to the step that opened them. The first holds [MitzvahLibraryList](../../components/MitzvahLibraryList.tsx) with neither `showMine` nor `navigable` (the registry mitzvot of the chosen nusach, Chok LeYisrael only once installed, the hilulot and Rosh Chodesh rows and the at-least-one-reminder rule; no screen outside onboarding and no add button), the second [ZmanimPicker](../../components/ZmanimSheet.tsx), the list of home's + sheet and Settings › זמני הלכה. An onboarded user never sees either: the root layout's guard sends every onboarding route home.
- Every step draws its progress with [OnboardingDots](../../components/OnboardingDots.tsx) and `ONBOARDING_STEPS` (six: welcome 0, nusach 1, location 2, bundles 3, permissions 4, ready 5); a new step raises that constant and shifts the later steps' indexes. Where a step's list `ScrollView` sits right above the dots, the dots take `marginTop: spacing.lg` and the list's content container `paddingBottom: spacing.lg` (the user's call, 2026-10-26).

## Local Contracts

- Onboarding state is stored through [../../stores/AGENTS.md](../../stores/AGENTS.md).
- What left the setup (spec A2): the marital status and the family purity opt-in are an offer on home after setup (`profile` and `taharah` of [../../utils/offers.ts](../../utils/offers.ts); the second only for a user `taharahOffered()` holds for, so a single user never meets the topic) and stay in Settings › Profile; the phone number is in Settings › Profile; the accuracy notice is the ready step's card.
- The gender is optional (App Review guideline 5.1.1(v), the owner's call, 2026-11-08): the welcome step's Continue is never disabled, its label says "(optional)", and an unanswered gender reads as the code already handles `null`: the siddur as printed, the default palette, the default mitzvot (tefillin on), candle lighting ticked. It goes through `chooseGender()` of [taharahOptIn.ts](../../stores/taharahOptIn.ts), never straight to the stores: it sets the library defaults (no tefillin or Kiddush Levana for a woman), the starting palette (`paletteForGender()`) and `siddurVoice` on every change of gender. The nusach goes through `chooseNusach()`.
- Location and permission behavior must use [../../services/AGENTS.md](../../services/AGENTS.md) wrappers.
- No step requires a permission or blocks on one (App Review 5.1.5, 2026-11-12): Continue on the location and notification steps is never disabled, and a refused location leaves the default place, Jerusalem (`CITIES[0]`), with `locationStatus` `denied` or `missing`, which home's `home.noLocation` banner then names. A button that leads to a system permission prompt reads Continue (`common.continue`), never Allow, Enable or Grant (5.1.1(iv)); the location and CTA suites fail on such a word in the English step. The setup can be finished on a Saturday: the ready step shows the names in a holy block and its sample only says why none came.
- User-facing copy must stay in [../../i18n/AGENTS.md](../../i18n/AGENTS.md).

## Work Guidance

- Keep permission explanations accurate to [../../../app.json](../../../app.json) native permission strings and actual behavior.
- Do not assume Expo Go can verify native notification/MMKV behavior.
- The mitzvot detail screen's rows are the shared [MitzvahLibraryList](../../components/MitzvahLibraryList.tsx), never [ChoiceRow](../../components/ChoiceRow.tsx) or a local list.
- A single choice on a step is [ChoiceRow](../../components/ChoiceRow.tsx) (the nusach) or [ChipRow](../../components/ChipRow.tsx) (the gender), each inside a `radiogroup` view named by its heading. No step hand-rolls either; the bundles step's ticks are `checkbox`es, because several hold at once.
- Every step's Continue, the ready step's finish and its sample button are the shared [Button](../../components/Button.tsx). The labels above the welcome step's fields are `field` [SectionLabel](../../components/SectionLabel.tsx)s.
