# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own pure helper logic for timeline, history, weekly report, liturgical-day, custom-mitzvah, Chok LeYisrael portion and reader text, prayer compass, and taharat hamishpacha (niddah cycle) calculations.

## Ownership

- [buildDayTimeline.ts](buildDayTimeline.ts) owns day schedule item construction (`urgent` and `expired` only on the app day; given `enabledSince`, a mitzvah is listed on a day only when `trackedOn()` from [checkIn.ts](checkIn.ts) holds, the rule `judgeDay()` judges by, so the day view and the schedule never list an unmarked window that ended before the mitzvah was switched on, and always list a marked one) and `currentOrNextWindow()`, which checks yesterday too, because night windows (omer, maariv) stay open past midnight, and looks up to nine days ahead past skipped windows (Sukkot in Israel keeps tefillin off for a week). It steps the location's calendar days from the app day (`appDay()`), each the device-local midnight of that date with zmanim at the location's noon, so the date it returns keys the same day as that window's notification. `dayWindow(mitzvah, date, location, settings)` is the one way to read a mitzvah's window on a day (`null` when it has none or `isSkippedAt()` skips it) and returns a `DayWindow` (`start`, `end`, `date`); `appDayWindow()` is the window home shows: one open now, whatever day it was computed for (maariv after midnight, a window still open past alot), else the one whose start falls in this app day (the day's own, or the next date's when it starts before alot), else none. A `multiDay` mitzvah (`Mitzvah.multiDay`, Kiddush Levana) is read `maxDays` days back by `appDayWindow()` and `currentOrNextWindow()`, so its window stays the one home shows on every night and day of its period, and `currentOrNextWindow()` looks `MULTI_DAY_LOOKAHEAD_DAYS` (45) days ahead for the next one instead of nine. `windowStillOpen(item, now)` tells whether an item's window has not yet ended: the day view and the schedule never call a past day's item missed while it holds. `windowDueToday(window, location, now)` tells whether a window is today's for a surface that opens a text with no date: it has not ended and starts no later than `appDayEnd()` of the current app day (open now, or opening before the next alot), so a window that opens later today and one that opens exactly at the app day's end count, while one that opens after it (candle lighting on a Wednesday, Shacharit from Shabbat noon, whose window opens at sunrise) does not; the reader, the siddur tab's rows and the mitzvah screen's open-text button pass a date only for such a window and leave the later day to `nextMitzvahTextWindow()`.
- [historyStats.ts](historyStats.ts) owns streak, daily, per-mitzvah, and missed-yesterday statistics. `judgeDayItems()` is the one judging loop: each item `done`, `late` (a done mark that `HistoryInput.late` flags, made after the time had passed), `missed`, `skipped`, `waiting`, `open` or `later`, with its window and mark time; `judgeDay()` is `verdictOf()` of it, which puts a `late` item in `missed`, so every percentage, calendar level, trend, the weekly report and every per-mitzvah run read a late mark as a miss (the owner's call, 2026-10-29), while `computeStreak()` keeps its day, because the mark is in `completions`; `createDayJudge()` memoizes by date key and `computeStats()` takes it as `extra.judge`. `judgeDay()` sorts every item of a day into done, missed or still open: a skip settles an item at once, and an unmarked window that ended before its mitzvah was switched on is not judged, while a done mark always counts (`trackedOn()`), so the morning of the install day, or of the day a mitzvah is switched on again, is never a miss and a mark made before it still counts. A day ends at `appDayEnd()`, the next alot, and outside a block an item stays open until both its day and its window have ended, so a window that runs past midnight (maariv, the Omer) is not missed while it can still be marked. Today's row, `missedYesterday` and the streak's walk start at `appDay(now, location)`, so between midnight and alot today is still the evening's date. `computeStreak()` walks back from today with no cap, through the archive past retention, to the oldest recorded day, and with nothing enabled stops at the first unmarked day. Each `perMitzvah` entry counts `done`, `eligible` (decided: done plus missed) and `pending` (still open), and `isTrackedInRange(stat)` (`eligible > 0 || pending > 0`) is the one rule for a mitzvah that was relevant on at least one day of the range; the history tab lists only those, so a mitzvah out of season (Sefirat HaOmer in Tishrei) never shows. A `multiDay` window is judged by its own end: it stays open until `window.end` (or its day's end, whichever is later), never waits in a check-in (`waitsInCheckIn()`), and a mitzvah switched on before that end is judged on it, not only one switched on before its first day ended. `computeStreak()` does not let such an item hold its day while it is open: the day is judged by its other items, so a miss shows in the streak at once instead of when the period ends, and a mark on the item keeps the day it is filed under.
- [historyTrends.ts](historyTrends.ts) owns what the history tab shows beyond the hero, pure over `HistoryInput` and a `DayJudge`: tallies (`due` = done + missed + skipped + open; waiting and later items are left out), the day, week and Hebrew-month period percents, the Hebrew-month calendar and its day levels, the 12 report weeks named by parasha or festival, the weekday chart with Shabbat and Yom Tov apart, 30-vs-30 per-mitzvah trends, the insight rules (a fall is never named), and the Hebrew and civil labels (`hebrewDayLabel()`, `hebrewRangeLabel()`, `civilRangeLabel()`, `formatCount()`, with no `Intl`).
- [badges.ts](badges.ts) owns the badge kinds, the mark totals with the archived counts (`markTotals()`, `markBadges()`, both leaving a late mark out, so it never counts toward the hundred and thousand marks badges), the forward fold (a full week or month needs every item `done`, so a late one blocks it, and a late item breaks its mitzvah's run as a miss does, while the day streak and its records keep the day) (`foldAchievements()`: walks from the stored fold checkpoint, commits it `FOLD_LAG_DAYS` (7) behind today and never past a day with an open item, re-reads `WEEK_RESCAN_DAYS` (14) weeks for the best week), the earned day of every badge from the data, `nextBadges()` and `earnedBadgeList()`. Persisting the result is the screen's call, through `saveAchievements()` in [../stores/useCompletionsStore.ts](../stores/useCompletionsStore.ts).
- [streakGoal.ts](streakGoal.ts) owns the history ring's goals: `STREAK_GOALS` (7, 30, 100, 365), `nextStreakGoal(streak)` (the first goal above the streak, then the next multiple of 365) and `streakGoalProgress(streak)` (the streak as a share of the next goal, the owner's call on 2026-10-21; 0 only at 0 days); a negative, fractional or non-finite streak counts as its whole days, never below 0.
- [checkIn.ts](checkIn.ts) owns the post-block check-in: which mitzvot it asks about (every window that reaches into the block, erev included, and one that ends exactly at the block's start counts as reaching into it, as Chok LeYisrael does on a Friday or an erev; but not one skipped or whose window ended before the mitzvah was switched on, `trackedOn()`), `blockForDay()` (a day's own block or the one it is the erev of), its deadline (`checkInDeadline(block, location)`: the alot that ends the first weekday after the block, `appDayEnd()` of that day, 04:00 on the location's clock where there are no zmanim) and `checkInPending(block, checkIns, now, location)`, which tests it, `latestCheckIn()`, and the pending ids the screens show as waiting. `trackedOn(mitzvah, window, enabledSince, done)` is the one rule for whether a window counts for a mitzvah (the user's calls, 2026-10-25): a window with a done mark always counts and shows as done, whenever the mark was made, even before the mitzvah was last switched on; an unmarked window counts only when the mitzvah was switched on before the window ended, while it could still be done, so the install day's morning, and the days before a mitzvah was switched off and on again, are never a miss, and a window still open when it is switched on (an evening's maariv, Selichot past alot, a `multiDay` period) counts; `judgeDay()`, `buildDayTimeline()`, `checkInFor()` and home ask it. `waitsInCheckIn(mitzvah, window, block)` is `overlapsBlock()` less a `multiDay` window that ends after the block, which stays open past it instead of waiting there; `checkInFor()` and `judgeDay()` both ask it.

- Keep utilities pure: no direct Zustand, MMKV, navigation, notifications, or native side effects.
- Accept required services/data as inputs instead of importing route state.
- Preserve local-date and halachic-location assumptions from [../services/AGENTS.md](../services/AGENTS.md) and [../data/AGENTS.md](../data/AGENTS.md).
- [buildDayTimeline.ts](buildDayTimeline.ts) and [historyStats.ts](historyStats.ts) take location dates held as device-local midnights, the day `dateKey()` names. They resolve zmanim and the day's observance from `locationNoon(day, location)`, never from the day's device-local midnight, which is the previous day at a location west of the device.
- Zmanim can be unavailable: [buildDayTimeline.ts](buildDayTimeline.ts) returns an empty timeline and [historyStats.ts](historyStats.ts) records a zero-eligibility day when `getZmanim()` returns `null`.
- Never re-implement the `skipOn` check. Any surface that decides whether a mitzvah applies must call `isSkippedAt()` from [skipRules.ts](skipRules.ts), or the surfaces disagree with each other.
- An item is missed only once it can no longer be marked: the end of its day (the next alot, `appDayEnd()`) or of its window, whichever is later, or the close of its block's check-in (the alot that ends the first weekday after the block). Percentages count only decided items, and the streak treats an open day as neither kept nor broken. Shabbat and Yom Tov have no special case beyond that.
- Never re-implement the quiet window either. The scheduler and the Shabbat screen both read `quietBlockAt()`; its edges stay open so the candle-lighting reminder and havdalah fire.
- [chok.ts](chok.ts) decides nothing about the calendar itself: holy days come from `HebcalService.isHolyDay()`, the quiet block from `quietBlockAt()`, the learning day's alot from `HebcalService.hebrewNightAt()` (its 04:00 fallback included), Thursday's nightfall from `HebcalService.nightfall()`, and the location day from `locationNoon()` and `civilHebrewDayAt()`. A change to any of those rules goes there, never into a copy in [chok.ts](chok.ts).
- Chok LeYisrael product decisions, confirmed by the user on 2026-10-12. Each lives in one named place in [chok.ts](chok.ts), and the assets carry both haftarah variants and every slot, so flipping one is a code change plus a dated test in [__tests__/chok.test.ts](__tests__/chok.test.ts) or [__tests__/chokText.test.ts](__tests__/chokText.test.ts), never a rebuild:
  - Israel or abroad follows the user's `inIsrael` setting (`chokParashiyot(shabbat, inIsrael)`), because the weekly parasha splits between Israel and abroad when a second day of Yom Tov falls on Shabbat, and every other surface of the app already reads the reading from the same setting. To follow the location instead, pass `location.inIsrael` at the callers.
  - The haftarah is the Sephardi one for Edot HaMizrach and the Ashkenazi one for Ashkenaz, Sefard and Chabad (`HAFTARAH_VARIANT`), because the source prints exactly those two variants and has no Sefard or Chabad variant of its own, so the nuschaot of Ashkenazi origin take the Ashkenazi one. To let the reader choose, replace the map lookup with a persisted user field.
  - Thursday night (`thuNight`, ליל שישי) counts for the day the user names (the user's call, 2026-10-25): `chokDoneDay()` files it under Thursday or Friday by `useUserStore.chokThuNightDay`, which the reader asks once; it has no window, make-up fold or streak day of its own, and in a Vezot Haberakhah week it files under Thursday, since that Friday has no portion. To give it a day of its own, add one in `chokDoneDay()` and a window in `CHOK_LEYISRAEL`.
  - The Friday of a Vezot Haberakhah week has no portion (`VEZOT_SLOTS` drops `fri`), the user's call made when the source printed none; the Torat Emet asset carries that Friday (Mishnah to Mussar, no Torah), so giving it a portion is a change to `VEZOT_SLOTS` and its dated tests, not a rebuild.
- Every decision about which liturgical insert applies on a day goes through `dayFeatures()` and `matchesCondition()` in [siddur.ts](siddur.ts). Never add a second copy of a day rule elsewhere.
- The [taharah/](taharah/) engine stores nothing: events are the only input and every state, perisha onah and task is derived by replay, so a corrected rule is a replay, not a migration. It never reads a device-local getter for a halachic question: days are `abs` numbers and instants resolve in the location's zone.
- Judge the skip at the mitzvah window's own `start` instant, after `computeWindow()`, never at "now" and never at civil midnight. `HebcalService.isShabbat` is instant-sensitive, so any other instant makes the answer depend on when the caller happened to ask.
