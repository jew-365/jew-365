# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

- Own the notification schedule's pipeline: store state is read once into a `PlanInput`, pure planners turn it into `ScheduleCandidate`s, and one adapter hands them to expo-notifications.

## Ownership

- [ids.ts](ids.ts) owns every constant (task names, categories, actions, notification kinds, MMKV keys, limits, `ANDROID_CHANNELS`, `SCHEDULE_FORMAT`), the `PendingNotificationMeta` payload type, and the pure id and payload helpers: `buildId(mitzvahId, date, idx, occurrence)` (an `occurrence` above 0 is written `${idx}.${occurrence}`, the n-th further trigger of a `blockExit` reminder), `parseId()` (the index read as an integer), `parseDateKey()`, `pendingNotificationMetaFromContent()`, `notificationTargetFromData()` and `notificationMatchesTarget()`.
- [types.ts](types.ts) owns `ScheduleCandidate`, `ChannelKey` and `PlanInput`. None of them mentions expo. `PlanInput.quietHours` is optional (absent means no quiet hours) and `readPlanInput()` sets it.
- [snapshot.ts](snapshot.ts) owns `readPlanInput(now, scope?)`, the one place a plan reads the stores, `currentScope()` and the `PlanScope` a caller may override, plus the small store readers the facade shares (`enabledMitzvot()`, `currentSettings()`, `hasNotificationPermission()`).
- [plan.ts](plan.ts) owns `buildPlan(input)`: runs the planners, thins the mitzvot when the full plan exceeds the cap, sorts by trigger, applies the platform cap less one slot and adds the end notice.
- [planners/horizonEnd.ts](planners/horizonEnd.ts) owns `planHorizonEnd(input, lastFullDay)`: the one `horizonEnd:<YYYY-MM-DD>` notice on the `system` channel (no category, `data.kind = 'horizonEnd'`), at 20:00 on the location's clock on the last fully planned day or at the exit of the block that covers that hour, dropped when that instant has passed; a tap opens home. Under quiet hours its 20:00 is clamped into the open hours (`min(max(1200, before), after)`), a time that falls in quiet hours (the exit of a block, the fallback) moves to the first hour on that date or the next, or with no first hour to the next date at the clamped hour, and a holy block at the moved instant drops the notice; it has no day bound, because it is the only warning that the reminders run out.
- [planners/horizon.ts](planners/horizon.ts) owns the horizon (`horizonDays()`: `HORIZON_MAX_DAYS` (30) location dates, the first two stretched through a block, `holyBlocksWithin()`, `recentDaysBefore()`, `horizonFor()`), `noticeTriggerAt(noon, timing, location)`, the instant a Rosh Chodesh or hilula notice fires for a `NoticeTiming` (the shkia plus a lead, or a clock time on the location's clock), `noticeDayLabel(opens, from, language)`, the weekday an evening opens on, with its date from seven days out, `noticePhase(trigger, opens, location)` (`NoticePhase`: `ahead` before the shkia of the civil day `opens`, or where it has none, `opening` at it, `open` after it), which the hilula and Rosh Chodesh planners word their text by, and `formatClock()`.
- [planners/mitzvot.ts](planners/mitzvot.ts) owns mitzvah reminders: `planMitzvot(input, horizon, thinFrom?)` (with `thinFrom`, every day from that date on keeps per window the one latest trigger at or before the window's end, never a `followUp` or `blockExit` one unless the window has nothing else, then its earliest trigger), `buildTriggerTime(reminder, window, location)`, `buildTriggerTimes()` and `bodyForReminder()`, and the per-window rule they share with the mitzvah screen's preview. `buildTriggerTime()` adds `offsetMin` minutes to the window's `start`, or to its `end` for an `end` or `followUp` reminder; for a `clock` reminder `offsetMin` is minutes after midnight on the location's clock, and the trigger is the first such wall-clock instant in the location's zone (Luxon) at or after the window's `start` when that falls inside the window, so a window of about a day files the time under the window that holds it, else the first one at or after `HEADS_UP_MAX_MIN` (180) minutes before the `start` (a heads-up), on the next day when that time is earlier still, with the wall clock kept across a DST change; a wall time a spring-forward day skips resolves an hour later, and the earlier of the two on a fall-back day (pinned in [../__tests__/reminderLogic.test.ts](../__tests__/reminderLogic.test.ts)). `buildTriggerTimes()` returns one trigger for each holy-block exit inside the window plus `offsetMin` for a `blockExit` reminder (`blockExitsWithin()`), and `[buildTriggerTime()]` for any other; `buildTriggerTime()` itself gives a `blockExit` reminder the window's end plus `offsetMin`, which only orders the mitzvah screen's rows, and a `WindowTrigger` carries the `occurrence` that `buildId()` writes. It decides nothing else: `reminderFires()` still drops a trigger more than 180 minutes before the window, after its end (a `followUp` one: at or before the end or more than `FOLLOW_UP_MAX_MIN` (720) minutes after it), or inside a holy block, so a clock reminder falls away on a day whose window ends before it. `windowTriggers(mitzvah, reminders, date, scope)` is that rule, in one place: the day has a window, `isSkippedAt()` does not skip it, it is not marked done or skipped, and each reminder whose trigger passes `reminderFires()`; `planMitzvot()` builds its candidates from it, over the horizon days plus the days before the first one, `PAST_WINDOW_DAYS` (2) or, for a `multiDay` mitzvah, its `maxDays` (16 for Kiddush Levana), so a window that opened yesterday and is still open after midnight (a custom mitzvah set across midnight) keeps its remaining reminders when a rebuild runs after midnight, and so does a follow-up up to 720 minutes after a full-day window that opened the day before yesterday; those days' past triggers fall to `reminderFires()`. A clock heads-up that the day before's window already fires at the same instant (a 03:00 clock reminder when the day before's window runs past 03:00 and the day's own window, too short to hold its next 03:00, reaches it as a heads-up; pinned with a fixture window in [../__tests__/NotificationScheduler.nextReminder.test.ts](../__tests__/NotificationScheduler.nextReminder.test.ts)) is left to that earlier window, the one open at that instant, so it is scheduled once: `windowTriggers()` drops it from the later day, but only while the earlier day still fires it, so marking or skipping the earlier day leaves the later day's own. Only a `clock` heads-up is compared. `nextReminderAt(mitzvah, reminders, scope)` walks the location's calendar days from two days ago (a `multiDay` mitzvah: `maxDays` ago) through ten days from today (`NEXT_REMINDER_LOOKAHEAD_DAYS`; a `multiDay` mitzvah: `MULTI_DAY_LOOKAHEAD_DAYS`, 45) through `windowTriggers()` and returns the earliest trigger strictly after `scope.now` over the whole range, or `null`: a later window's heads-up can come before an earlier window's last reminder. Its `ReminderScope` is the `PlanInput` subset `now`, `location`, `settings`, `completions` and `skipped`. It is not a planner and reads no store; the facade and the web shim both export it, and `buildTriggerTime()` too. Two screens call it: the mitzvah screen's preview, with the real marks, and the reminder editor's save check, with empty ones, where `null` for a draft means it would never fire. A candidate's `data.skipIfDone` is `skipsIfDone(reminder)`, true for every `followUp`; `bodyForReminder()` words an English `followUp` as `reminder.followUp.label` with the English name instead of `notifications.timeFor`, and a reminder with no label and no variants (a follow-up saved empty) from `reminderLabel()` with the mitzvah name in the UI language, so the body is resolved when the notification is built and is never empty. For a mitzvah with `bodyKey` and `bodyParams`, the Hebrew body reads the keyed line only when the chosen text holds no `%{key}` placeholder and is the registry's own wording (`isRegistryText()`: a `label` or `bodyVariants` entry of the mitzvah's `defaultReminders`, its Hebrew or English name, which the reminder editor saves for an empty label, or an empty text, compared trimmed); any other text is the user's and goes out through `interpolate()` with the params. The English body is always the keyed line. Under quiet hours (`ReminderScope.quietHours`, optional like `PlanInput.quietHours`) `ownTriggers` moves each planned trigger through `quietHoursTrigger()` before `reminderFires()` judges it, with `latest` the window's end, or its end plus `FOLLOW_UP_MAX_MIN` (720) minutes for a `followUp` reminder, the very ranges `reminderFires()` accepts; a `WindowTrigger` carries `moved` (its trigger differs from the planned instant), and `planMitzvot()` returns `withoutMovedDuplicates()`: candidates of one mitzvah at one instant are grouped, only a group of two or more with at least one moved member is reduced, to the member of the later `dateKey`, then the lower `reminderIndex`, then the first in order, so a coincidence with nothing moved stays as it is. The `nextReminderAt()`, `thinned` and `buildTriggerTime()` signatures are unchanged.
- [planners/holyBlock.ts](planners/holyBlock.ts) owns the pre-block notice with its Omer lines and its Ushpizin lines, one per night inside the block while `ushpizin` is in `input.mitzvot` (enabled, and the user's nusach says it), naming that night's guest in the UI language; both read the same evenings (`eveningsWithin()`: the erev's and every day's but the last, whose night opens at the exit).
- [planners/hilulot.ts](planners/hilulot.ts) owns the hilula notices, read from `input.hilulaNotices`, and `hilulaLines()`, the lines the pre-block notice carries for the notices a block swallows; an `evening` notice reads `bodyTonight` while `ahead`, `evening.body` at `opening` and `evening.titleBegun` / `bodyBegun` once `open`. A notice's `data.hilula` is `{ day, when, ids }`, `ids` being the ids of the tzaddikim it names in the order of `HILULOT` (the order of the names in its text): the tap in [../notificationResponseHandler.ts](../notificationResponseHandler.ts) opens `/hilula/[id]` on the first, with all of them as `ids` when there are two or more, and `/hilulot` for a notice with no known id, as one scheduled before the pages existed. `ids` is optional in `PendingNotificationMeta` for that reason, so `SCHEDULE_FORMAT` is not raised. Under quiet hours each notice first moves through `quietHoursTrigger()` with `calendarDayEnd()` of its planned time as the latest, and takes its phase, so its wording, from the moved trigger; `hilulaLines()` reads the planned trigger.
- [planners/roshChodesh.ts](planners/roshChodesh.ts) owns the Rosh Chodesh notices (`before`, `evening`, `day`), read from `input.roshChodeshNotices`, and `roshChodeshLines()`, the lines the pre-block notice carries for the ones a block swallows. The day arithmetic is [../../data/roshChodesh.ts](../../data/roshChodesh.ts). A `before` or `evening` notice that is `open` takes `roshChodesh.notice.begun.*`, a `before` notice one day ahead that is not `open` takes `before.bodyTonight`, and `day` always keeps `day.*`. Under quiet hours each notice first moves through `quietHoursTrigger()` with `calendarDayEnd()` of its planned time as the latest, and a `before` or `evening` notice takes its phase, so its wording, from the moved trigger (`day` stays `open`); `roshChodeshLines()` reads the planned trigger.
- [planners/checkIn.ts](planners/checkIn.ts) owns the check-in nudges and `checkInInputOf()`: the block's exit, two hours later and `lastCallFor()`, 20:00 on the location's clock the day after the block (half an hour before the next block's candle lighting when that evening is already holy), none at or after the check-in's deadline (`checkInDeadline()`, the alot that ends the first weekday after the block).
- [planners/report.ts](planners/report.ts) owns the weekly report notice (`planReport()`, `REPORT_DELAY_MIN`): one per block that closes a week, 45 minutes after its exit. Which block closes a week is `weekEndFor()` in [../../utils/weeklyReport.ts](../../utils/weeklyReport.ts). Under quiet hours the trigger moves through `quietHoursTrigger()` with `calendarDayEnd()` of the planned time as the latest, before the past and holy-block checks.
- [planners/taharah.ts](planners/taharah.ts) owns the taharah reminders, the merged pre-block notification and `bedikotMissingIn()`. `TAHARAH_QUIET_POLICY` gives every task kind its rule under quiet hours, run by `quietTrigger()` before `beforeQuiet()`: `exempt` (`hefsek`, `bedikaEvening`, `tevila`, `tevilaPrep`, `perisha`, `bedikaVeset` and `preBlock`: never moved, each a same-day act with a deadline or the instant a separation begins), `moveOrKeep` (`bedikaMorning`: moved to the first open hour only while that is not after the task's own end, else it keeps its time) and `move` (`expectOnset` and `postBlock`: moved up to `calendarDayEnd()`, else dropped).
- [os.ts](os.ts) owns every call into expo-notifications: channels, categories, scheduling (`schedule()` one by one, `sync()` the difference (the facade replaces everything instead on a stale format, once a day and on Android's first rebuild of a process) against `getScheduled()` by `contentHash()`, a FNV-1a hash of title, body, trigger, category, channel and payload that `requestFor()` stores as `data.contentHash`), cancelling, the tray, permissions, the foreground handler and the action task registration.
- [__tests__/plan.test.ts](__tests__/plan.test.ts) pins `buildPlan()` on fixed inputs.
- [__tests__/notices.test.ts](__tests__/notices.test.ts) pins the Rosh Chodesh and hilula notice kinds through `buildPlan()`: each kind off, its days ahead and its timing, the text for each distance and on each side of the opening shkia in Hebrew and English, `noticePhase()`, and the pre-block lines.
- [__tests__/quietHours.mitzvot.test.ts](__tests__/quietHours.mitzvot.test.ts) pins the quiet hours on mitzvah reminders through `buildPlan()` and `nextReminderAt()`: the move to the first open hour, the drop at a window's end, follow-ups, windows past midnight, the duplicate rule and the agreement of the preview with the plan.
- [__tests__/quietHours.notices.test.ts](__tests__/quietHours.notices.test.ts) pins the quiet hours on the Rosh Chodesh, hilula and weekly report notices (the move, the drop, the reworded phase), `TAHARAH_QUIET_POLICY` and the morning bedika, and that the pre-block notice and the check-in reminders never move.

## Local Contracts

- The flow is one-way: `readPlanInput()` → `buildPlan()` → `os.schedule()`. The facade orchestrates it under the lock; no planner calls the facade or [os.ts](os.ts).
- A planner is a pure function of `(PlanInput, Horizon)`. It never calls a store's `getState()`, never reads the clock (`new Date()`, `Date.now()`; the instant is `input.now`) and never imports expo. Text comes from `t()`, which follows the live locale; `input.language` only chooses between a mitzvah's, a hilula's or a Rosh Chodesh month's Hebrew and English names and the language of a weekday name, which comes from `formatDate()` ([../../i18n/index.ts](../../i18n/index.ts)), never from Luxon's `toFormat`.
- `PlanInput` holds each fact once. The completion, skip and check-in maps are read by the mitzvah planner directly and assembled into the check-in input by `checkInInputOf()`; there is no second copy of the marks.
- A candidate names its channel by `ChannelKey`. `os.schedule()` builds the request: a date trigger carrying `channelId`, `autoDismiss: true`, `sticky: false`, `sound: 'default'` and the candidate's category when it has one. A notification's channel is chosen where it is planned, as the table in [../AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) lists.
- `buildPlan()` plans block notices, check-ins, the weekly report, taharah, hilulot, Rosh Chodesh, then mitzvot, sorts by trigger (stable, so equal instants keep that order) and cuts at `planCap(platform) - 1` (`IOS_MAX - IOS_HEADROOM` on iOS, `PENDING_LIMIT` elsewhere), warning about what it drops. The horizon is `HORIZON_MAX_DAYS` days, so the cut is what bounds it. When the full plan holds more than the room, the mitzvot are planned again thinned from `horizon.thinFromIndex` (the third day, or later when the opening stretched through a holy block); the other planners are never thinned. The end notice (`planHorizonEnd()`) takes the last slot, on the last day the cut left whole, or the horizon's last day when nothing was cut; when the cut lands so near that this time has already passed, it goes out at the last kept trigger (at least five minutes from now, and at the exit of a block that covers it). Without permission the plan is empty.
- A failure costs only its own candidates, because `cancelAll()` has already run: block notices, check-ins and mitzvot isolate each block or each mitzvah and day inside their planner; `buildPlan()` guards the weekly report, taharah, hilula and Rosh Chodesh planners as a whole.
- Quiet hours (`PlanInput.quietHours`) are applied where each planner builds its trigger, through `quietHoursTrigger()` in [../../utils/skipRules.ts](../../utils/skipRules.ts) only, before that planner's past and holy-block checks; `planBlockNotices()` and `planCheckIns()` never read them. Ids, payloads, categories and `SCHEDULE_FORMAT` are unchanged: a moved trigger changes only `contentHash` and the choice of body variant.
- [os.ts](os.ts) hands the facade `TrayNotification`s (`identifier`, parsed `data` and, for one in the tray, `presentedAt`, the delivery date, which expo-notifications reports in seconds on iOS and in milliseconds on Android and `getPresented()` reads by magnitude), never expo objects. It makes no decision: suppression, mark-done, the tray rules, the update notice policy, permission state and the lock stay in the facade.
- A presented mitzvah reminder (no `data.kind`, a target from `notificationTargetFromData()`) leaves the tray once its app day has ended: at or after `appDayEnd()` ([../../utils/locationDay.ts](../../utils/locationDay.ts)) of its `dateKey` day, and not before `appDayEnd()` of the app day it was presented on, so a follow-up after its window and a later trigger of a multi-day window stay through the day they came on. `reminderDayEnded()` in [../NotificationScheduler.ts](../NotificationScheduler.ts) is the one rule: `dismissCompletedPresentedNotifications()` applies it at launch and on every return to the foreground, beside the done, skipped, check-in, taharah and update rules, and `DAILY_REBUILD_TASK` applies it alone at the start of every run. A block has no exception: what it swallowed waits in its check-in. Every other kind keeps its own rule. Android drops new notifications once an app holds about 50 in the tray. Pinned by [../__tests__/NotificationScheduler.endedDay.test.ts](../__tests__/NotificationScheduler.endedDay.test.ts).
- [ids.ts](ids.ts) is the only home of the payload type and the kind constants. A new kind of notification adds its kind and payload field there, builds its candidate (id included) in a planner, and follows the checklist in the root [../../../AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

## Work Guidance

- A planner takes `(input, horizon)` and returns candidates; register it in `buildPlan()` in the order its ties should resolve.
- Read a new fact in [snapshot.ts](snapshot.ts) and add it to `PlanInput`; do not reach for a store from a planner.
- Test a planner through `buildPlan()` with a hand-built `PlanInput` and `at()` from [../../testing/zmanim.ts](../../testing/zmanim.ts). The clock is in the input, so no timer faking is needed.
